F02–F05 are Miledge-level foundation and infrastructure-alignment stages. They must not silently become detailed PEWA product design or engineering. The sequence remains F01 → F02 → F03 → F04 → F05; changing stage scope or order requires explicit Founder authorisation.

**Working method:** Discuss → Challenge → Refine → Agree → Document → Founder disposition → Proceed.

F01 has Founder approval; F02 is closed by the [F02 disposition](PEWA-F02-COIF-001-Founder-Disposition-and-Closure.md). F03 was explicitly authorized to start for Founder review on 16 October 2026 and is closed by the [F03 disposition](PEWA-F03-PAP-001-Founder-Disposition-and-Closure.md); F04 was reviewed and is closed by the [F04 disposition](PEWA-F04-ECD-001-Founder-Disposition-and-Closure.md); F05 is closed by the [F05 disposition](PEWA-F05-PCES-001-Founder-Disposition-and-Closure.md). Each stage closes only when its stated output has been reviewed and the Founder records an explicit disposition (approve, approve with conditions, return for revision, reject, or defer). A supporting research package may inform a stage but cannot replace its objective, output, review or approval gate. PEWA-F02-R01 research and the approved v0.1 classification reference were supporting evidence for F02; the explicit Founder disposition closed F02. The R01 materials do not themselves authorize a Miledge capability/domain position, and the F02 disposition does not approve admission, Domain, Shared Platform, final architecture or implementation.

No application implementation, engine implementation design, conversational journey specification or infrastructure deployment is authorised by this programme. Transition to the PEWA project home required, and has now received, a separate Founder disposition ([PHT-001](PEWA-PHT-001-Founder-Transition-Disposition-and-Closure.md)) confirming the approved Miledge baseline, handover of unresolved items and project authority. These are future project-home activities, not part of the current F02 assessment or this programme closure. FEF adoption does not replace PEWA's project authority, and no implementation begins without separate authorization.
