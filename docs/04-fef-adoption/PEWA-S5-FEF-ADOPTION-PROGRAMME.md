## 2. Proposed bounded workstreams

### FEF-01 — Authority and baseline inventory
Locate and read the effective FEF Adoption Guide, work-package/change-control standard, relevant repo/branch/PR/test/merge controls, Miledge platform policies and any prior product adoption examples. Record exact source links, revisions, approval states, overlap and applicability. Where FEF source is unavailable, mark as unverified rather than synthesising a substitute standard.

**Deliverable:** controlled baseline/source register and applicability map.

### FEF-02 — PEWA domain and architecture adoption mapping
Map 314 requirements and 41 approved policies to Stage 4 logical boundaries, architecture risks and FEF governance evidence obligations. Preserve product authority over orders/units, protected content and guest access, published-menu independence during unit exhaustion and merchant image responsibility.

**Deliverable:** requirements-to-governance matrix identifying materially critical controls and unowned gaps; do not build giant item-by-item tables without practical review value.

### FEF-03 — Engineering work-package discipline
Assess how future work will specify bounded implementation outcomes, dependencies, acceptance criteria, tests, evidence, branch/PR governance, independent review, exact-head checks and explicit merge permission. Derive practices from **verified** FEF controls, not memory-based guesses.

**Deliverable:** PEWA engineering-work-package charter/template proposal and handoff review gates (documents only).

### FEF-04 — Infrastructure and shared-service readiness
Check PEWA candidates and constraints against effective Miledge architecture/media/CI guidance. Verify rather than assume the shared WhatsApp adapter's authority and readiness, and identify decisions for PostgreSQL, identity, hosting, image storage/delivery, search, observability and payments.

**Deliverable:** owner-specific dependency/evidence/decision register; no provider selection or provisioning absent distinct authority.

### FEF-05 — Security, privacy, commercial and launch-policy blockers
Identify which unresolved numerical policies, consent/guest recovery details, jurisdictional legal work, customer/business terms and ad viewability thresholds are hard blockers to *engineering a specific feature* versus blockers only to live launch. Stage 5 must not quietly make commercial or legal decisions.

**Deliverable:** sequenced blockers with Founder/Miledge/legal owner and exact decision trigger.

### FEF-06 — Readiness review and Founder disposition
Consolidate baseline fit, remaining actions, admissible engineering sequencing and recommended work-package start criteria. Determine whether Stage 5 can close conditionally while preserving later feature-level policy gates. Create separate Founder closure proposal; **Stage 6 requires subsequent independent initiation regardless of Stage 5 closure**.

**Deliverable:** final Stage 5 readiness assessment and formal Founder closure candidate, each separately reviewed as warranted.

## 3. Readiness acceptance questions

1. Is each invoked FEF standard verified by authoritative source, effective version and PEWA applicability?
2. Is PEWA's requirement/architecture authority intact with no invented implementation or provider obligations?
3. Are critical invariants testable in later work packages, notably one business acceptance/one unit, concurrency, 200 monthly, purchased carry-forward, ten-unit outstanding cap, tenant isolation and protected order retrieval?
4. Can developers prove an exhausted business still has published menus, Home/Directory discoverability and manual contact throughout a current base subscription, with only new orders disabled?
5. Are public/protected menu indexing and associated image assets prevented from leaking across audience boundaries?
6. Are merchant-uploaded image rights/content representations and proportionate PEWA takedown paths allocated to future terms/policies?
7. Are Miledge WhatsApp and other shared services conditional on verified authority and contracts?
8. Are provider, commercial, privacy/legal, operational and release obligations assigned to the correct separate decision/implementation gate?
9. Are FEF work-package practices concrete enough to avoid undocumented scope expansion, self-approval or silent deployment?
10. Is an independent Stage 6 authorisation still required before any engineering begins?
