# Reusable versus Industry-Specific Capability Evaluation

MPA-002 treats an enduring capability as stable across industries and implementations; an Industry Implementation adapts reusable infrastructure to a sector. The reusable capability hypothesis is sector-neutral: structured organisational offerings publishing, access, discovery and optional structured selection/ordering. Food service is the initial reference context, not commercial validation of wider applicability. The distinction below is prospective and does not expand PEWA F01 scope, approve a general commerce platform or allocate a final MPA layer.

## Boundary model

- **Reusable capability hypothesis:** sector-neutral structured organisational offerings publishing, access, discovery and optional structured selection/ordering. This remains prospective and commercially unvalidated; it does not expand PEWA F01 or approve a general commerce platform.
- **Food-service industry implementation:** PEWA's initial reference context, with menus, food categories, portions, modifiers, availability and food-service fulfilment semantics. Existing F01 sectors remain unchanged.
- **Organisation configuration:** each organisation controls its offerings, display names, grouping/presentation and service-specific variations. This content authority is separate from technical responsibility for storing and publishing records; final canonical data authority and tenancy remain open.
- **End-user experience:** web, QR and optional WhatsApp entry and interaction are experience/channel expressions of the capability. They do not define the domain data authority or require a particular provider or implementation.

## Reuse threshold for the next decision

A capability should not be called a Miledge Shared Platform or service solely because more than one product could use it. Before proposing a layer, document the recurring need, distinct consumers, existing Domain/Platform/service/module candidates, extension feasibility, unique capability responsibility, accountable owner, evidence maturity and consequences of duplication. Apply MPA-002's escalation order; a new layer requires explicit authority and stronger evidence.

## Evidence limits

PEWA F01 supplies the approved initial contexts and boundary hypotheses. The F02-R01 consultant assessment and v0.1 classification reference establish useful food-menu examples, not cross-industry demand, market scale, engineering readiness or Miledge portfolio economics. Those stronger claims remain unverified.
