## 3. Canonical product understanding retained for handover

- PEWA provides business-authored public and protected menu infrastructure, independent web and QR access, Directory and Home return/discovery, manual engagement and optionally enabled structured ordering.
- English is PEWA's primary platform navigation language; business-created names, categories and descriptions may use **any language**. French platform navigation is optional future work. Optional food classifications assist search and do not force fixed categories, translations, ingredient/allergen assertions or English equivalents.
- A standard monthly organisation subscription includes **200 accepted-order units** even for a menu-only business. Unused monthly included units expire at period end; purchased top-up units carry over subject to disclosed terms. Additional unit bundle rates, US$10/month and US$0.05/unit are **illustrative, not final**.
- Structured ordering requires deliberate business enablement. A request is Submitted before explicit business Accepted; each valid acceptance consumes one unit. Ready is optional and business-declared. The **10 recoverable units** are a separate, bounded outstanding organisational obligation for legitimate previously admitted commitments.
- Paid Featured Offers purchase eligible delivered on-screen impressions, independent of organic relevance and not sales outcomes. Organic Directory is not pay-to-rank.
- Guest access remains protected without a compulsory account; PEWA web status is primary; browser push optional; outbound WhatsApp status disabled by default. WhatsApp integration requires later Miledge shared adapter authority.
- PEWA operates integrity, subscription/usage support, bounded cases and customer protection but not merchant acceptance, cooking, fulfilment or customer meal-payment processing. Operator Console capability is defined now; full operator experience assembled last.
- Approved policies must inform later **Business Terms, Customer Terms, Privacy Notice and Platform Policies** and receive competent jurisdiction-specific review prior to live use.

## 4. Closure conditions and reserved gates

**Proposed Stage 3 completion** means the reviewed product requirements baseline and Founder policy directions are sufficiently coherent for a separately authorised Stage 4 architecture assessment. It **does not** certify:

1. **Final commercial authority** — prices, taxes, monetary conversion, Enterprise contracts, validity periods, grace times, recovery debt/corrections.
2. **Consumer commitment mechanics** — exact order expiry bounds, overlapping availability precedence, cancellation statutory rights, last-unit races.
3. **Protected identity/privacy readiness** — hotel audience evidence, secure guest recovery, lawful data retention/deletion, breach handling and country-specific compliance.
4. **Campaign measurement readiness** — qualifying viewability threshold, invalid/repeat traffic, credits/refunds and targeting constraints.
5. **Miledge shared provider readiness** — WhatsApp adapter status, provider/network implementation and any reserved portfolio classification.
6. **Implementation readiness** — tests, services, technical designs, security controls, providers, deployment or live Terms/Privacy consent.

These are **assigned to Founder product/commercial policy, competent legal/privacy review, Miledge shared-platform authority and later architecture/release gates as applicable**, before implementation or real customer charging and processing. Later authorities may not silently contradict the approved product baseline. If a substantive open issue changes product semantics, return it to Founder decision and amend the requirements under control.
