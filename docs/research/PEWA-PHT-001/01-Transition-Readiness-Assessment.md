## Genuine transition blockers

**None found.** A blocker is an unmet prerequisite in F05 D7 or a missing authority needed to establish the project home. The following were considered and are *not* blockers:

| Item | Why not a blocker |
|---|---|
| No Constitution, Product Definition or Requirements exist | They are the stages that follow the transition. |
| No `FEF-ALIGNMENT.md` | FEF Adoption Guide Stage 2 is a project-home activity; adoption is step 5 of the sequence and separately authorised. |
| Miledge admission/classification unresolved | Preserved as unresolved by F02–F05; the gate is satisfied by the *approved position* (prospective, evidence-gated), see note below. |
| MWA readiness, data authority, tenancy, subscriptions, order exceptions, consumer protection, hotel access | F05 D7 explicitly makes these non-blockers; they are allocated in document 4. |
| Market evidence for F01 hypotheses | Unvalidated by design; allocated to Product Definition/research. |

## Notes for the Founder

- **Interpretation to confirm.** The gate text refers to "the approved Miledge baseline/position". The only approved Miledge-facing position is F02's prospective, hypothesis-only treatment (no admission or classification). This assessment reads the gate as satisfied by that position plus the inspected Miledge and FEF baselines above. If the Founder intended an actual Miledge admission or classification first, that would be a different, Miledge-authority gate and would hold the transition.
- **Housekeeping, not blockers.** The README still describes PEWA as in "foundation/discovery", and the Foundation Programme is framed as the foundation programme only. These should be updated when the transition is dispositioned. Local `.DS_Store` files are untracked and unrelated.
- **Baselines.** The Miledge and FEF mains are unchanged from the F04/F05 inspections. A new approved baseline in either before the transition would warrant re-confirmation.
