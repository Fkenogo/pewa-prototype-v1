# PEWA-S3-COM-001 — Standard Subscription and Consumption Policy Decision Brief

**Status:** FOUNDER APPROVED — COM-D01–D07; approved 18 October 2026 (PR #29), merged into main. Exact commercial prices remain unapproved.  
**Date:** 18 October 2026  
**Authority:** Approved R05 as revised in [PR #28](https://github.com/Fkenogo/pewa/pull/28), R04, Stage 2 Product Definition, and [Stage 3 reconciliation](PEWA-S3-REC-001-Consolidated-Requirements-Reconciliation.md).  
**Scope:** Product and commercial policy only. No provider, billing technology, architecture, implementation or final price approval.

## 1. Decisions already established

- A **standard organisation subscription**, renewed monthly, covers menu listing, discovery, manual engagement and **200 accepted-order units**, whether or not the business enables ordering.
- A valid **business acceptance** consumes one unit; browsing, submission, withdrawal before acceptance, decline and expiry do not.
- Businesses may add **unit top-up bundles** without changing plans.
- **Enterprise** supports individually agreed commercial conditions for larger organisations while keeping PEWA order semantics and privacy protections.
- Separate **10 recoverable consumption units** provide bounded continuity and create recoverable debt, not free additional included units.
- Featured Offer exposure is a separately purchased service.
- **US$10/month**, **US$0.05/unit** and **US$10 for a 200-unit top-up bundle** are illustrations only; no final local-currency prices/taxes/FX have been approved.
- Standard subscription lapse after notice and grace restricts new public listing/engagement but not existing history or legitimate bounded order-resolution access. Running out of ordering units alone does not remove a current paid menu.

## 2. Founder-approved decision set (recommended options approved in PR #29)

### COM-D01 — Included monthly units and renewal

**Approved direction:** At each successful standard monthly renewal, grant that subscription period's 200 included units. Unused **included** units expire at the period boundary rather than accumulate indefinitely. Distinguish unused included units from separately purchased top-ups.

**Alternative:** Allow a bounded carry-forward period/cap for included units to reduce perceived wastage. Requires explicit limit and communication.

**Review:** A menu-only business still receives the allocation, but is not compelled to activate ordering; expiration of unused units is not a penalty on its listing.

### COM-D02 — Purchased top-up balance

**Approved direction:** Unused purchased top-up units survive standard subscription renewal, while becoming usable for new structured orders only when the relevant subscription/ordering mode is eligible. Set a separately disclosed expiration policy, rather than silently expiring purchased units monthly.

**Alternative:** A fixed purchase-validity period with clear up-front disclosure.

**Open policy:** precise validity duration (if any), closure treatment, lawful refunds and whether top-ups can transfer to an Enterprise contract.

### COM-D03 — Unit consumption priority

**Approved direction:** Consume monthly included units first, then purchased top-up units, then bounded recoverable continuity allowance **only where valid pre-existing engagement policy permits**. Show the source of consumption and remaining balances to the organisation.

**Alternative:** Prioritise earliest-expiring paid balance first; this may reduce waste but complicates the promise of 200 included monthly units.

### COM-D04 — Ten recoverable unit eligibility and debt

**Approved direction:** The ten units remain a **maximum outstanding recoverable allowance per organisation**, not a fresh ten-unit grant on every monthly renewal. They cover eligible already-admitted outstanding order commitments when ordinary balances are insufficient. Usage creates an attributable debt obligation; restoring paid capacity requires governed debt settlement/offset. Do not allow unlimited repeated overdrafts through renewal cycles.

**Open policy:** how recovery obligations are settled (deduct from future paid units, cash settlement, or customer-visible charge to organisation), treatment during payment grace, simultaneous acceptances near the last unit, and correction/dispute cases.

**Important boundary:** Do not block access to protected status or lawful customer resolution merely because an allowance reaches zero; distinguish actions requiring an additional accepted-order unit from status/cancellation communication.

### COM-D05 — Subscription overdue, suspension and reactivation

**Approved direction:** Separate renewal-due, overdue grace, suspended and reactivated states. A suspended business retains saved content, permitted commercial settings and lawful existing-order resolution; a new paid period restores eligible public listing without rebuilding content. A top-up is **not** a substitute for renewing the base subscription.

**Open policy:** grace duration, failed-payment retry, warning timing, effective renewal date and whether subscription days are prorated after restoration.

### COM-D06 — Enterprise invariants

**Approved direction:** Enterprise pricing, monthly included volume, allocation, payment conditions and support can be negotiated. The meanings of Submitted, Accepted, Ready, customer protection, historical records and PEWA's non-merchant role cannot be negotiated away. Enterprise requires an attributable agreement and clear organisation/brand scope.

**Open policy:** eligibility, quotation authority, contract duration, and treatment of standard-plan unused balances on transition.

### COM-D07 — Communication and billing display

**Approved direction:** Present one understandable standard offer rather than making businesses choose between a listing plan and a usage plan. Show: current subscription state; 200 included monthly units, separately purchased units, recoverable allowance/debt and consumed acceptances; top-up pricing and billing currency; and which ordering contexts are explicitly enabled. Never imply that all 200 units were used by a menu-only business.

**Open policy:** exact commercial rates, taxes, currency conversion, regional payment methods and receipts require separate decisions.

## 3. Worked examples (illustrative only)

- **Menu-only café:** current monthly subscription, business publishes menus and uses zero accepted-order units. No separate ordering charge or additional plan.
- **Ordering café:** 200 accepted orders in its period use the 200 included units. A 201st *new request* must pass the applicable available top-up/eligibility rules; the ten-unit continuity allowance is not an unlimited substitute for top-up.
- **End of period:** unspent included units expire under recommended D01; bought top-ups retain validity under recommended D02; the next successful standard renewal grants 200 new included units.
- **Existing pending orders:** if balances deplete after a customer submitted an eligible request, business may still resolve the valid commitment within approved bounded recoverable rules.
- **Large hotel group:** may negotiate Enterprise volumes and terms, but its order status and privacy meanings stay identical.
