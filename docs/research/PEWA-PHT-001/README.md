This package assesses whether PEWA may move from its completed Foundation Programme (F01–F05) to its dedicated product-home programme. It does not repeat foundation research, start any project-home stage, author the Product Constitution, admit or classify PEWA within Miledge, adopt FEF, or authorise engineering.

## Conclusion

**Recommended outcome: AUTHORISE WITH CONDITIONS.** No genuine transition blocker was found. Four conditions are proposed (document 5). The transition would not start any project-home stage; each stage still needs its own authority.

## Controls

The transition gate used is the one the Founder approved in [MP-ALIGN-001](../../00-foundation/MP-ALIGN-001-Master-Programme-and-Miledge-Infrastructure-Alignment.md) and [Foundation Programme](../../00-foundation/FOUNDATION-PROGRAMME.md), as amended by F05 Condition A. Approved F01–F05 dispositions are not modified. This package is a proposal; the Founder transition disposition is the only thing that can authorise the transition.
