## Conditions

- **Condition A — project-home sequence.** The future project-home sequence is Founder-amended to: (1) Product Governance Foundation / Constitution; (2) Product Definition; (3) Requirements development and approval; (4) Product Architecture and technical decisions; (5) FEF adoption/readiness confirmation; (6) separately authorised engineering. FEF standards may inform earlier stages; final engineering readiness remains a separate authority gate. This amends only the future project-home sequence; each stage still requires its own authority.
- **Condition B — public versus protected access.** As recorded under D2. No access-control mechanism, identity design or detailed experience is selected.
- **Condition C — entitlement continuity.** As recorded under D3. Entitlement governs capability availability, never business-event meaning. No subscription plan or enforcement design is approved.

## Boundary of this closure

This disposition closes F05 only. It does **not** authorise or approve the project-home transition, any project-home activity (Constitution, Product Definition, requirements, Product Architecture), FEF adoption or readiness, provider selection, architecture selection, deployment or implementation. It does not amend canonical Miledge architecture, admit PEWA, assign portfolio classification, or approve an Enduring Capability, Domain or Shared Platform. Approved F01–F04 records are unchanged.

The project-home transition requires a separate Founder disposition confirming the approved Miledge baseline, the accepted handover and project authority.
