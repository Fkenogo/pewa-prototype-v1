# Founder Decision Brief

## Recommendation

Approve the bounded-configuration, business-primary, entitlement-governed strategy in this package as PEWA's F05 foundation direction, accept the handover register as the transition input, and keep the project-home transition behind a separate Founder disposition.

## Proposed strategy in brief

- **Configuration** ([doc 2](02-Business-Configuration-Strategy.md)): businesses configure identity and presentation, offerings and publication, service context, declared availability, engagement mode within entitlement, and approved contact preferences. PEWA fixes meaning and trust rules. Subjects only; no settings, schemas or flows.
- **Experience** ([doc 3](03-Product-Experience-Strategy.md)): eight principles XS-01–XS-08 covering onboarding, publishing, persistent access, discovery, manual engagement, ordering, declared outcomes and public/protected information.
- **Channels and entitlement** ([doc 4](04-Channel-and-Entitlement-Alignment.md)): web independent; WhatsApp optional and MWA-conditional; both modes entitlement-governed; ordering also needs enablement; no plans or providers.
- **Consistency** ([doc 5](05-Cross-Context-Consistency-and-Variation.md)): constant meaning across contexts; variation admitted only by a five-part test.
- **Transition** ([doc 6](06-Project-Home-Transition-Readiness-and-Handover.md)): handover register in eight groups; the approved sequence is preserved, with a proposed (not yet effective) amendment adding a Product Architecture step.

## Principal trade-offs

See the table in the [executive assessment](01-Executive-Strategy-Assessment.md): configurability vs consistency, onboarding friction vs platform trust, persistent access vs control, entitlement strictness vs continuity, hotel breadth vs narrow domain, handover completeness vs duplicated governance.

## Unresolved handover items (headline)

Canonical data authority and tenancy; cancellation/correction/amendment authority; customer-data authority and per-class lifecycle/privacy; reporting/moderation/appeals; subscription, billing and entitlement implementation and lapse policy; hotel restricted access; MWA readiness and other shared-service evidence; FEF alignment record. These are carried to named later project-home stages and are not automatic pre-transition blockers.

## Proposed decision statement

Subject to Founder disposition, PEWA's product configuration and experience strategy is: businesses configure their own facts and permitted choices within entitlement; PEWA fixes shared meaning, truthfulness, public/protected treatment and platform accountability; web access is independent and WhatsApp optional; and the transition to PEWA's project home follows the approved sequence only after a separate Founder disposition. F05 remains open pending this decision.
