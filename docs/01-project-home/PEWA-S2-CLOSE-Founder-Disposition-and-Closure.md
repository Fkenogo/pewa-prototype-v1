## 2. Accepted Product Definition

The Founder-approved Stage 2 definition covers:
- Menu-first proposition; participating food-service businesses, hotels, service contexts and delivery-only businesses.
- Accountable organisations with legitimate public brands, multiple locations/menus and scoped team permissions; progressive onboarding.
- Public and business-configured protected menus; business-controlled guest menu and ordering access.
- Offering management, business-declared price/availability and context-specific single-currency orders.
- Independent public web access, QR/direct links, optional conditional WhatsApp, PEWA Home, recents/favourites, optional PWA and customer identity.
- Search across eligible public offerings, business brands, categories, tags and service areas; organic search separate from paid featuring.
- Menu-only/manual engagement and optional entitlement-plus-business-enabled structured ordering, business-declared acceptance/readiness, controlled exceptions and protected guest order access.
- Local-currency-first PEWA business charges; organisation subscription; accepted-order consumption; business-owned offers; delivered-impression-based Featured Offers.
- Bounded existing-journey continuity including **10 recoverable consumption units**; subscription lapse affects new visibility/engagement, not content/history retention.
- Primary PEWA in-app/web notifications, outbound WhatsApp disabled absent separately justified approval.
- PEWA-observed reporting, personal-data protection, bounded Operator Console intervention (Console assembled last).

Working commercial figures (**basic entry reference ceiling US$10/month and ~US$0.05 per accepted order**) are **not final approved rates**. PEWA does not process customer food-order payments or guarantee promotional conversions.

## 4. Continuity and guardrails

The Constitution's historical integrity and accountability rules apply: business-declared facts and customer order outcomes are not replaced by platform guesses; lawful correction and erasure remain possible. Subscription changes do not destroy customer or business historical meaning. Business content remains business-supplied; the Operator Console is not a merchant operations console.

The exact product treatment of subscription lapse versus temporary continuity under the 10 recoverable-unit allowance must preserve **both** no abrupt termination of an already-valid engagement and no indefinite free public listing. The approved Product Definition's delineation controls unless later explicitly refined.
