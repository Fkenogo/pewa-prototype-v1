# PEWA-F02-R01-C — Starter Classification Catalogue v0.1

**Status:** Founder-approved v0.1 practical reference: 34 optional, non-exclusive entries. It is not exhaustive or mandatory. The IDs are document-local references only; they are not product identifiers or a data model.

## How to read the catalogue

- Entries classify a broad food or beverage identity. They do not define a business’s menu layout.
- The list is intentionally flat. Entries are not exclusive; an item may fit more than one useful classification, and no single primary parent-child path is required.
- “Examples” illustrate likely fit, not a canonical definition of a culture, dish, ingredient or preparation.
- The **regional examples** cell is explicitly provisional where source terminology conflicts or lacks local validation. It is not a synonym dictionary.
- Preparation can be part of the best-fitting classification where useful (for example, grilled fish); course, occasion, item format, options and attributes remain separate dimensions.
- Businesses may use a broader entry, choose another entry, create a local classification, or leave the shared match blank.

## Provisional regional examples and cautions

These source-reported examples can help initial businesses find a broad fit, but they are not validated shared synonyms or fixed recipes:

- **Matooke / matoke / igitoki:** Candidate under FD-05 when described as a cooking-banana/plantain staple. Consultant 2 groups Matooke with cassava under “Tubers” despite describing Matooke elsewhere as green banana. Preserve the term supplied by the business; do not auto-equate Matooke and cassava.
- **Isombe / sombe / pondu:** Candidate under FD-04 or FD-09 based on the business’s description of the actual dish. Consultants 3–5 generally place the leaf preparation with vegetables/stews; Consultant 6 D2 FAM-R06 lists “Isombe” under freshwater fish synonyms while its D4 names isombe separately from isambaza. This source conflict is retained; no shared synonym is established here.
- **Mukeke, ndagala, isambaza:** Source-reported freshwater-fish names; preserve as individual business terms. Do not assume they refer to the same species or menu item.
- **Brochette, mishkaki, nyama choma:** Source-reported grilled/skewered terms that may fit FD-03. Local naming, ingredients and preparation may differ.
- **Chapati, injera, ugali/ubugali, cassava products:** Choose FD-05 when presented as a staple food, FD-11/FD-12 where the described item is a filled or topped format, or FD-14 as a bakery item. That is a fit suggestion, not a mandated role.

## Deferred extensions

The following are intentionally not shared v0.1 classifications: cuisine and country indexes; service-period or course labels; children’s menus; “chef’s choice” or popularity labels; combos and bundles as category families; retail/pantry goods beyond the F01 food-and-beverage menu scope; therapeutic diet categories; exhaustive species, ingredients, preparation methods or local synonym dictionaries; and regional categories whose source evidence is conflicting. Businesses may still use any of these as menu categories or item descriptions. Composite offers remain sellable items made from components, not automatically food identities.
