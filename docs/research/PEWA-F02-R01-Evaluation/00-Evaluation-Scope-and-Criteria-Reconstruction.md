## Founder-authorised evaluation methodology

The six cross-cutting evaluation criteria and weights authorised by the Founder are:

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Coverage of eight research areas A–H | 20% |
| African and regional food relevance | 20% |
| Classification consistency and usability | 20% |
| Tags, attributes, dietary safety and search | 15% |
| Research evidence quality and traceability | 15% |
| Multi-location reuse and business customisation | 10% |
| **Total** | **100%** |

### Evidence-based scoring scale

| Score | Meaning |
|---:|---|
| 0 | Absent/unassessable |
| 1 | Minimal |
| 2 | Partial with significant weaknesses |
| 3 | Adequate with gaps |
| 4 | Strong |
| 5 | Comprehensive and well-supported |

For each criterion, the evaluator must give an evidence-based rationale and cite the supporting consultant source. A score describes the evidence available in the submission; it does not itself approve a classification or safety claim.

**Weighted total = SUM(score/5 × criterion weight).** Weights are applied as percentages (or equivalent fractions); the resulting total is on a 0–100 scale.

Areas A–H are research coverage areas. The six weighted criteria are cross-cutting evaluation dimensions that may draw on evidence from multiple A–H areas. There is no one-to-one mapping between the areas and the criteria, and none is implied here.

Regardless of aggregate score, each evaluation must explicitly report missing deliverables, evidence confidence, unsupported claims, food-safety issues, and regional classification errors. These findings must remain visible and traceable even when an aggregate score is high.
