# PEWA-F03-PAP-001 — Founder Decision Brief

**Decision sought:** disposition the proposed F03 principles for use as the approved Miledge-level constraints carried into later PEWA foundation stages. This brief does not itself create a Miledge architectural position or authorize F04/F05.

## Recommended decision

Approve the principles with the following explicit conditions:

1. Business authority over supplied offering meaning, content, prices, availability and business outcomes remains distinct from the technical system that stores, validates or publishes records. Canonical technical state authority stays open.
2. Later architecture must enforce secure organisation isolation, least privilege and product-domain authorization. F03 chooses no tenant model, identity provider or role structure.
3. Reusable structured offerings remain a hypothesis. Approved PEWA scope and exclusions in F01 remain controlling; no general-commerce expansion follows from the hypothesis.
4. MWA remains the intended, optional shared WhatsApp boundary only after current contract, implementation conformance, operations, access and PEWA readiness are independently evidenced. Web access must remain independently available.
5. Infrastructure follows approved product/domain requirements. Portability seams are proportionate to risk; no database, hosting, provider or deployment architecture is selected.
6. Privacy, lifecycle, audit, recovery and operations are required later decision topics, with specific policies and targets assigned to later authority.

## Why this is the recommended balance

It carries forward the decisions F01 and F02 actually made while preserving the decisions they explicitly left open. It uses MPA-002 and MPA-005 for architecture sequence and authority boundaries, and MTAIP-001 for risk-proportionate provider neutrality. It avoids treating repository code or a contract as proof of an operating shared service, and keeps optional WhatsApp failure from becoming a product access failure. It leaves engineering choices until approved requirements exist.

## After disposition

If approved, update the appropriate F03 disposition record through a separate controlled action, then begin F04 only under the Foundation Programme's stage sequence. If Miledge-specific positions are desired, obtain those through MPA authority. Do not treat this package itself as approval, F03 closure or implementation authority.
