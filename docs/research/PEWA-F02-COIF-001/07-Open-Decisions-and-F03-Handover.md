## Proposed F03 handover considerations — only after F02 disposition

If Founder closes F02 with an approved capability/ownership position, F03 may use the approved position and unresolved items to establish Miledge-level platform architecture principles. Potential inputs include provider neutrality, portability proportional to risk, data ownership, tenancy/isolation, integration boundaries, operational responsibility, security/privacy and shared-service maturity evidence (MPA-002; MTAIP-001; MV-206; MV-207). These are handover topics, not F03 decisions or a proposed detailed architecture.

Keep project-specific workflows, role matrices, ordering journeys, UI, schemas, APIs, provider choices and implementation architecture for the separately governed PEWA project home after F05 and the transition gate. F03 must not be started by this record.

## F02 closure condition

The PEWA Foundation Programme requires review and explicit Founder disposition of the F02 output. This assessment remains pending that disposition; no closure is claimed.
