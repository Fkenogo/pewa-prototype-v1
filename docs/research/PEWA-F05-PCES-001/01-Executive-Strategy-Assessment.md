# Executive Strategy Assessment

## Recommendation

Adopt a **bounded-configuration, business-primary, entitlement-governed** product strategy:

1. **Businesses configure what is theirs.** A participating business controls its identity, offerings, publication, declared availability and service context, its choice of engagement mode within its entitlement, and its permitted contact preferences.
2. **PEWA controls what must stay consistent.** The meaning of statuses, truthful presentation, public-versus-protected treatment, isolation, authorization, report handling and entitlement enforcement are product rules. A business cannot configure them away.
3. **Subscriptions govern capability, not meaning.** Both operating modes are entitlement-governed. Structured ordering additionally needs business enablement. Entitlement decides *whether* a capability is available, never *what it means*.
4. **Experience follows a small set of principles, not designed journeys.** Eight principles (document 3) fix direction for onboarding, publishing, persistent access, discovery, manual engagement, ordering, declared outcomes and public/protected information, leaving journeys and screens to Product Definition.
5. **Web is the stable foundation; WhatsApp is an optional, conditional channel.** No experience principle depends on WhatsApp.
6. **Hand over, do not continue.** F05 ends with a practical handover list. Closing F05 does not authorise the project-home transition; that needs its own Founder disposition. The package also proposes, for Founder decision, adding an explicit Product Architecture step to the project-home sequence; it is not effective unless approved.

## Why this shape

- It is the least that satisfies F01 (business is primary; two modes; persistent access), F03 (organisation isolation, configuration before bespoke variation, channel independence) and F04 (eight areas, entitlement-plus-enablement gate, PEWA accountability for semantics and reports).
- It keeps the reuse hypothesis testable. Variation expressed as bounded configuration over stable shared meaning is the evidence that would later support or refute sector-neutral reuse ([document 5](05-Cross-Context-Consistency-and-Variation.md)).
- It stops at strategy. Settings, schemas, roles, flows and screens are deliberately not invented.

## Principal trade-offs

| Tension | Strategy position | Cost accepted |
|---|---|---|
| Configurability vs consistency | Configure presentation and business facts; fix meaning and trust rules. | Some businesses will want variation PEWA will not offer without a product decision. |
| Onboarding friction vs platform trust | Publication requires a defined readiness condition (what it contains is for Product Definition). | Slower first publication; risk of over-gating small businesses. |
| Persistent public access vs business and customer control | Published offerings are deliberately public and stable; everything else defaults to protected. | Unpublishing, caching and search-index behaviour must be resolved later. |
| Entitlement strictness vs continuity | Entitlement is enforced in meaning; what happens on lapse is left open (document 4). | Historical order outcomes are protected from silent rewriting; menu visibility, active-order completion, downgrade, grace and suspension are left to Product Definition and Requirements. |
| Hotel/food-service breadth vs narrow domain | Hotels are in scope; service-context variation is configuration of declared information, not new logistics. | Room-service and delivery are described, never operated. |
| Handover completeness vs duplicated governance | One consolidated handover; no re-litigation of F01–F04. | Some items appear as pointers, not full analysis. |

## What this package does not decide

Settings and their schemas, onboarding steps, publish workflow, discovery ranking, subscription plans, price, channel interactions, provider choices, data lifecycle rules, order exception handling, canonical data authority, tenancy, or any technical architecture.
