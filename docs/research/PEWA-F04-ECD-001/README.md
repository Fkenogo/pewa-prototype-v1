## Authority and scope controls

The F01–F03 decisions are carried forward without reopening them. Any proposal here is subordinate to their controlling dispositions. This package leaves canonical menu/order technical authority, tenancy mechanism and Miledge layer mapping unresolved. Food, beverage and meal-related offerings remain the approved PEWA scope; wider reuse is an unvalidated hypothesis. Web access remains independent of optional messaging.

**Founder review corrections (PEWA-F04-ECD-001-CORR-001):** this revision reconciles six review findings: PEWA ownership of food-service domain meaning; a data-class responsibility inventory; manual-engagement facilitation; unallocated cancellation/amendment authority; PEWA report-handling and consumer-protection accountability; and the two-part (entitlement plus enablement) gate for structured ordering. The eight capability areas are unchanged.

F04 was closed by the Founder disposition recorded in [the F04 closure record](../../00-foundation/PEWA-F04-ECD-001-Founder-Disposition-and-Closure.md). No F05 activity, product configuration strategy, experience design, engineering, provider selection, integration, deployment or Miledge admission/classification is included.
