## Working principles

Business menus remain business-branded and persistent. PEWA provides access, shared discovery and optional engagement/ordering infrastructure without taking over kitchen, restaurant management or delivery operations. PEWA integrates with Miledge shared WhatsApp infrastructure rather than duplicating WhatsApp provider connectivity.

Founder review is required before promoting foundation drafts to approved Product Truth. Work proceeds **F01 → F02 → F03 → F04 → F05**.
