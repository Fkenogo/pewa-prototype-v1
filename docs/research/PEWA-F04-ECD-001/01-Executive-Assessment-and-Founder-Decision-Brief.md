# Executive Engine Capability Assessment and Founder Decision Brief

## Recommendation

Define the PEWA engine as a product-owned set of domain capabilities that lets participating food-service businesses maintain structured offerings; lets people access and discover those offerings over persistent web destinations; facilitates the approved manual customer-engagement mode (menu access with business contact, no order recorded in PEWA); and, where the business is both entitled under its subscription and has enabled it, records structured customer selections or orders and communicates business-declared outcomes truthfully. Both F01 operating modes are governed by subscription entitlement.

Treat the eight capability areas in this package as a capability boundary for assessment, not as a commitment that they must be one Miledge Enduring Capability, one Shared Platform, or a single indivisible engine. Keep the F02 sector-neutral reuse position as a working hypothesis. The food, beverage and meal-related scope and F01 exclusions remain controlling.

PEWA may validate, present, publish and record product outcomes. A business retains authority over the meaning and correctness of its supplied content, prices, availability, order acceptance, readiness and fulfilment, and owns its conversations with customers in manual engagement. PEWA is accountable for accurate system behavior and for not claiming an outcome it has not established, for the food-service domain meaning of its product (its food-service specialisation is internal to PEWA, not an independent owner), and, at platform level, for handling reports of misleading or problematic published content and meeting applicable consumer-protection obligations. The business's responsibility for content accuracy does not remove that accountability. Authority for cancellations, corrections and amendments is not allocated and remains for later definition. Technical record ownership is separate and unresolved.

## Proposed boundary

| PEWA engine responsibility | Participating business authority |
|---|---|
| Provide product behavior for structured offerings, publishing, persistent access/discovery, manual-engagement facilitation and, where entitled and enabled, selection/order recording. Enforce the meaning of subscription entitlements at capability level. | Supply and maintain offering content, prices and availability; choose which offerings to publish; conduct manual customer engagement; choose whether to enable ordering within its entitlement; decide whether to accept or decline an order and declare readiness; perform preparation, service, collection and delivery. |
| Validate received data against product rules, make the source and publication state clear, and preserve record integrity for actions PEWA performs. | Decide business meaning, policy and commercial terms represented by those records. |
| Communicate the recorded order state and actor accurately; distinguish customer-submitted order from business acceptance and business-declared readiness. | Make the actual acceptance and readiness declarations. |
| Maintain security, authorization, isolation, privacy and integrity obligations within the product behavior it provides; handle reports of misleading or problematic published content and meet applicable consumer-protection obligations. | Authorize business content and operational decisions within the business; remain responsible for the accuracy of supplied content. |

Cancellation, correction and amendment authority (customer- and business-initiated) is deliberately left unallocated. This division does not resolve which technical system is canonical for menu or order records, who has write/publish authority at the storage layer, or how organizations are isolated. F03 explicitly leaves these subjects open.

## Capability conclusion

The strongest candidate responsibilities are A structured catalogue, B publishing/access/discovery with manual-engagement facilitation, and C optional structured selection/order. D order recording and truthful status communication is a bounded responsibility only where the business enables ordering. E bounded organization configuration supports differences without changing shared meaning. F keeps food-service rules distinguishable from reusable capability while leaving them PEWA product logic. G preserves channel-independent domain behavior. H expresses cross-cutting product obligations for access, authorization, integrity, truthful outcomes, report handling and consumer protection.

Potential grouping for future Miledge assessment is by recurring human/business need, not by current product name or technology. The evidence does not justify declaring one Enduring Capability or Shared Platform. The capabilities may be assessed together initially, then separated only if distinct recurring needs, independent reuse, materially different authority or lifecycle, or divergent business meaning are demonstrated.

## Alternatives and trade-offs

| Alternative | Benefits | Costs and risks | Assessment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Treat PEWA as a food-service-specific capability set | Strong fit to approved scope; fewer claims about generality; easier to preserve food-service meaning. | May duplicate common publishing/access behavior across operating contexts; underplays F02's approved reuse hypothesis. | Credible fallback if cross-context evidence remains weak. |
| Treat all eight areas as one sector-neutral Enduring Capability | Simple portfolio narrative and coherent customer/business proposition. | Risks conflating cataloguing, publication, selection, order state, channel handling and security obligations; implies broader reuse and one lifecycle without evidence. | Do not adopt as a Miledge position now. |
| Preserve a bounded product capability set and test sectors separately | Keeps the food-service product coherent while exposing boundaries and evidence needs; no premature Miledge layer decision. | Requires later evidence to determine whether capabilities should be combined or separated in Miledge architecture. | Recommended for F04; Miledge disposition remains outside this package. |

## Founder decisions requested

1. Approve, conditionally approve, return, reject or defer the proposed PEWA engine boundary and responsibility split.
2. Confirm that business authority over content, prices, availability, order acceptance, readiness and fulfilment is distinct from PEWA's technical record, truthful-behavior and platform-accountability responsibilities, and that cancellation/amendment authority stays open.
3. Confirm that manual engagement is a facilitated PEWA mode, and that optional structured ordering is within PEWA only when the business is both entitled under its subscription and has enabled it, with PEWA recording the customer submission and business declarations without supervising fulfilment.
4. Confirm that persistent web access and discovery stand independently of WhatsApp or any other optional messaging service.
5. Confirm that the eight areas remain candidates for capability assessment and do not establish one Miledge Enduring Capability, Infrastructure Domain, Shared Platform, Shared Service or layer mapping.
6. Confirm that canonical menu/order technical authority, tenancy mechanism, and any shared-service adoption stay unresolved for later evidence and authority.
7. Confirm the data-class responsibility inventory (business/organisation, offering, customer, selection/order, media, operational/audit/telemetry) as capability-level responsibility only, with lifecycle and privacy decisions still open.
8. Identify any conditions or evidence required before closing F04 and permitting the separately controlled F05 stage.

## Proposed decision statement

Subject to the Founder’s disposition, PEWA’s candidate engine capability boundary is to manage business-supplied structured food, beverage and meal-related offerings for publication and persistent access/discovery, to facilitate manual customer engagement, and, for entitled and enabling businesses, to support optional structured selection/order recording and truthful communication of customer and business-declared outcomes. Businesses retain authority over business facts and decisions; PEWA owns the correctness and security of the product behavior it provides, its food-service domain meaning, entitlement meaning and platform accountability for reports and consumer protection. The boundary does not decide canonical technical data ownership, tenancy, Miledge portfolio architecture, product architecture, or implementation.

F04 must remain open pending this decision. F05 is the next stage only after the Founder records F04 disposition under the Foundation Programme.
