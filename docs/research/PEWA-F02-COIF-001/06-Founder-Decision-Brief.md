# Founder Decision Brief

**Requested disposition:** decide whether to accept this evidence assessment as a completed F02 work product for further review, request revisions, or defer. This brief requests no project admission, MPA classification, Shared Platform position, architecture or implementation.

## Recommended disposition

1. **Capability hypothesis:** endorse for further MPA review a sector-neutral enduring capability hypothesis for structured organisational offerings publishing, access, discovery and optional structured selection/ordering, using food service as PEWA's initial reference context. Broader applicability remains prospective and commercially unvalidated; this does not add sectors to approved F01 scope or approve implementation.
2. **PEWA domain responsibilities:** retain as a proposal that PEWA domain logic may validate offerings and manage product behavior for publication and enabled order recording/transmission. Do not approve canonical menu/order data authority, tenancy or MPA layer ownership here; these require explicit PEWA and Miledge decisions. Business authority over content, pricing, availability and fulfillment decisions remains distinct from technical storage/publishing responsibility.
3. **WhatsApp:** retain Miledge WhatsApp Adapter as the intended shared integration boundary, conditional on separately verified conformance, support, deployment, operating owner and PEWA readiness. Its current plan/artifact mismatch is an external Miledge reconciliation item and not automatically a blocker to PEWA F02 closure if treated as unresolved.
4. **Other shared services:** use evidence-first treatment. Confirm a named owner, current status, contract, operating evidence and PEWA access before recording identity, tenancy, media, search, subscription, analytics, audit or notification dependencies.
5. **Miledge architecture:** defer admission, Domain, Shared Platform and architecture dispositions to the proper Miledge authority and explicit Founder decision. This assessment itself authorizes none of them.

## Decisions requested

### 1. PEWA capability responsibility boundary

**Option A — retain F01 boundary as working proposal (recommended):** PEWA may own food-service domain logic for menu/order behavior. Do not assign canonical data authority, tenancy or technical storage/publishing ownership until explicit PEWA/Miledge disposition. Businesses control content, pricing, availability, acceptance, preparation and fulfilment decisions; that authority is distinct from the system that technically stores and publishes records. This preserves approved F01 while leaving Miledge layer placement open.

**Option B — reopen capability decomposition now:** ask for further evidence on whether publishing, discovery and ordering are separate enduring capabilities before any MPA layer proposal advances. Trade-off: sharper MPA description, additional F02 work and no near-term disposition.

**Option C — defer MPA mapping:** retain F01 only and do not recommend Domain/Platform relationships until further strategic evidence is available. Trade-off: avoids premature classification but leaves shared infrastructure fit less actionable.

### 2. MWA dependency posture

**Option A — conditional candidate (recommended):** retain the Miledge WhatsApp Adapter as the intended shared integration boundary, but do not describe it as PEWA-available until Miledge resolves implementation authority/maturity, accountable ownership, supported interaction evidence, deployment/operations and product access.

**Option B — architecture direction only:** record MWA as a portfolio design direction and exclude it from any near-term dependency plan pending formal Miledge disposition of current code/artifact status.

**Option C — permit a separately scoped readiness proof:** only after relevant Miledge/product authority approves a bounded non-production proof plan. This assessment itself does not authorize that proof.

### 3. Other candidate shared dependencies

**Option A — keep all unresolved (recommended):** require evidence of actual service owner, scope, contract, access and maturity before mapping identity/tenancy, media, search, subscriptions, analytics/audit or notification services.

**Option B — request a bounded portfolio inventory:** ask the relevant Miledge owner to identify existing services and status before a later decision. This creates a distinct evidence task and no provider/integration design.

### 4. Status of nine-layer proposal

Record all nine rows as evidence-only and defer any MPA architecture disposition to an explicitly authorised decision. This follows MPA-005's distinction between assessment proposal and architectural authority.

## Principal trade-offs

- A clear PEWA responsibility proposal supports F02 progress; calling it an authorised Miledge layer would exceed authority.
- MWA has materially stronger artifacts than other candidate dependencies, but artifacts do not resolve its authority and operations mismatch.
- An evidence-first boundary avoids designing product mechanics within Miledge-level F02 and preserves the separate project-home transition.

## No decision requested here

No request to approve PEWA admission, assign a Domain, approve a Shared Platform, select provider/architecture, implement or deploy an adapter, begin F03, modify F01/R01, or close F02. Those require separate authority and disposition.
