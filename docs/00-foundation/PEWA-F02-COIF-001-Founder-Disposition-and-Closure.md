# PEWA-F02-COIF-001 — Founder Disposition and F02 Closure

**Status:** Founder approved; F02 closed 16 October 2026
**Authority:** Explicit Founder approval of the five F02 recommendations, 16 October 2026
**Programme:** PEWA Foundation Programme, F02 — Capability & Domain Boundaries
**Assessment:** [PEWA-F02-COIF-001 assessment package](../research/PEWA-F02-COIF-001/01-Executive-Assessment.md)
**Traceability:** [Approved PEWA F01](PEWA-F01-Problem-Opportunity-and-Platform-Thesis.md); [PEWA-F02-R01 evidence assessment](../research/PEWA-F02-R01-Assessment/01-Submission-Inventory.md); [MPA-002 and MPA-005 authority inventory](../research/PEWA-F02-COIF-001/00-Authority-and-Evidence-Baseline.md).

This record closes PEWA F02 and records the Founder dispositions below. The assessment remains research evidence and proposed alignment material. This disposition does not approve PEWA admission, a Miledge portfolio classification, an Infrastructure Domain, a Shared Platform, final architecture, or implementation.

## FD-01 — Reusable Capability Hypothesis

**APPROVED AS WORKING HYPOTHESIS.**

PEWA will explore a sector-neutral enduring capability for structured organisational offerings publishing, persistent access, discovery and optional structured selection or ordering.

Food-service remains the initial reference context. Cross-industry applicability is prospective and commercially unvalidated. This decision does not expand approved F01 industry scope or authorise a general commerce platform.

## FD-02 — PEWA Capability Responsibility Boundary

**APPROVED AS PROVISIONAL BOUNDARY.**

PEWA is the candidate owner of product/domain logic associated with offerings publishing, discovery, customer access and enabled order behaviour.

Participating businesses remain responsible for their supplied content, pricing, availability, order acceptance and fulfilment decisions. Technical responsibility for storing and publishing records is distinct from business authority over their contents.

Canonical menu/order data authority, tenancy boundaries and final Miledge layer ownership remain unresolved and require later explicit decisions.

## FD-03 — Miledge WhatsApp Adapter

**APPROVED AS CONDITIONAL SHARED DEPENDENCY.**

The Miledge WhatsApp Adapter remains PEWA's intended shared WhatsApp integration boundary.

Actual adoption requires separately established conformance, operational ownership, deployment/availability, support and PEWA integration readiness. The existing MWA documentation/artifact maturity discrepancy is assigned to Miledge for reconciliation. It is not a blocker to this F02 closure.

No PEWA integration or Meta deployment is authorised.

## FD-04 — Other Shared Infrastructure

**APPROVED — EVIDENCE-FIRST TREATMENT.**

Media, identity, tenancy, search, subscriptions, billing, analytics, audit and notifications remain candidate shared infrastructure relationships.

Do not represent any candidate as an approved or operating shared service without evidence of its ownership, scope, contract, maturity and availability. Do not independently select or create these services through this closure.

## FD-05 — Miledge Architecture Position

**APPROVED — PROSPECTIVE ASSESSMENT ONLY.**

Accept the F02 nine-layer assessment as evidence for subsequent Miledge decisions.

No admission, portfolio classification, Infrastructure Domain assignment, Shared Platform declaration, final architecture or implementation is approved. Such decisions remain subject to MPA-002/MPA-005 and the appropriate Miledge authority.

## Closure and next stage

F02 is closed. F03 — Platform Architecture Principles — is the next eligible foundation stage and has **not started**. Starting F03 substantive work requires a separate task and authority. The approved F01–F05 sequence and stage boundaries remain unchanged.

The external MWA implementation-authority/documentation reconciliation remains with Miledge. Other conditional shared-service relationships remain subject to the evidence requirements in FD-04. These open dependencies do not reverse the F02 closure or authorise integration work.
