## Stage boundary

- **F03:** principles and constraints, unresolved decision register, and request for Founder disposition. No detailed design.
- **F04:** capability authority, responsibility boundaries and engine role at capability level. Do not decompose into services, schemas, endpoints or implementation.
- **F05:** high-level configuration and experience strategy across contexts, respecting F01 and F04. No journeys, screens or role matrices.
- **Later PEWA Product Architecture:** after F05 closure and separate Founder transition authority, resolve technical data authority/state, tenancy mechanism, identity/authorization, lifecycle behavior, dependency/fallback design, infrastructure and operations based on approved requirements.
- **Miledge authority:** any admission, portfolio classification, Enduring Capability, Domain, Shared Platform, Shared Service ownership or change to canonical Miledge architecture remains solely with the applicable Miledge/Founder authority. This proposal makes none of those decisions.
