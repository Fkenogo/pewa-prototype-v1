# PEWA-F02-COIF-001 — Executive Assessment

**Status: proposed evidence assessment for Founder review.** It does not authorise Miledge admission, a Domain or Shared Platform classification, architecture, or implementation. F02 remains pending.

## Principal findings

1. **PEWA has a clear candidate domain-responsibility hypothesis.** Approved F01 assigns PEWA menu/order business logic, hosting and presentation of business-supplied menus, customer access/discovery, and recording/transmission of enabled orders. F01 does not approve canonical menu or order data authority. This assessment proposes PEWA domain logic and product-controlled state as a candidate; the final canonical data authority, tenancy boundary and storage/publishing responsibility remain open for F02 and relevant Miledge decisions. Businesses retain authority over content, prices, availability, acceptance, preparation, food quality and fulfilment decisions; that business authority does not by itself decide which system stores and publishes records. [PEWA F01 §§2, 7–8]
2. **Reuse should be assessed before new infrastructure.** MPA-002 and MV-303 require a left-to-right capability assessment and reuse-first testing. MV-303 mentions Menu-as-a-Service as expected portfolio evolution; no approved PEWA position or operating menu platform is shown. [MPA-002 §§3–4, 8; MV-303 §§2, 7]
3. **The Miledge WhatsApp Adapter is the strongest identified shared relationship, but not an integration PEWA can currently be assumed to consume.** MV-207 approves an optional adapter direction and MWA-001/002 define a contract foundation. The canonical Miledge repository contains schema and Python implementation artifacts, tests, and CI workflows; the governing MWA-003A plan says it is not implementation authority, and documents do not establish provider transport, production deployment, operating readiness, or PEWA access. This authority/maturity mismatch is an external Miledge reconciliation item; it is not, by itself, a blocker to PEWA F02 closure if PEWA treats MWA use as conditional. [MV-207 §§1–4, 9; MWA-001 §§1, 13–14; MWA-002 §§1, 9–11; MWA-003A §§1–2]
4. **Several other shared relationships remain candidates only.** Identity/tenant conventions, media storage/delivery, subscriptions, search/discovery, analytics, notifications and audit are not evidenced as operating shared services for PEWA. MV-203 remains draft; MV-206 provides media guidance but no provider/service mandate. [MV-203 status; MV-206 §§1, 8–9; PEWA F01 §8]
5. **Reusable capability hypothesis:** structured organisational offerings publishing, access, discovery and optional structured selection/ordering may be sector-neutral, with food service as the initial reference context. PEWA F01 scope remains unchanged; wider applicability is prospective, commercially unvalidated and not approved for implementation. The approved F02-R01 package informs food-service examples but does not prove cross-industry demand or a general commerce platform case. [PEWA F01 §§3–4; F02-R01 Baseline §§1–4]
6. **Portfolio and project authority must stay separate.** This assessment is a proposed Mode B record, not the architecture disposition. No one of its nine layers is independently authorised. If F02 is later approved, F03 remains a separate programme stage; product workflows and engineering belong after the distinct project-home transition defined in the PEWA Foundation Programme.

## Recommendation for disposition

Recommend the sector-neutral structured-offerings capability hypothesis for MPA review, using PEWA's approved food-service scope as the initial reference only. Treat PEWA domain responsibilities as proposals; do not decide canonical menu/order data authority, tenancy or technical storage/publishing responsibility. Keep business authority over content, pricing, availability and fulfilment decisions distinct from technical responsibility for storing and publishing records. Retain the MWA as the intended shared integration boundary, conditional on verified conformance and readiness; record its implementation-authority/documentation mismatch as an external Miledge reconciliation item, not an automatic F02 closure blocker. Keep other shared services evidence-gated. Defer admission, Domain, Shared Platform and architecture positions to the proper Miledge authority and explicit Founder disposition.

## Strongest evidence and limitations

- **Strongest authority:** approved PEWA F01; approved MPA-002/MPA-005/MV-303; approved MV-207 and MWA-001; accepted MWA-002 contract foundation.
- **Strongest implementation evidence:** committed MWA schemas, adapter package source/tests and CI configuration at Miledge main `6a34a99…`.
- **Not established:** current passing CI results; complete behavioral conformance; deployed adapter; live Meta connectivity; service operations/on-call; access/credentials for PEWA; integration availability; service-level commitments; cost allocation or commercial fit.
- **Contradictory maturity signal:** implementation artifacts are committed while the MWA plans/contracts state those documents do not themselves authorise implementation. This is a Miledge documentation/authority reconciliation item; it does not establish current conformance, deployment, support or PEWA readiness, nor automatically block PEWA F02 closure.
