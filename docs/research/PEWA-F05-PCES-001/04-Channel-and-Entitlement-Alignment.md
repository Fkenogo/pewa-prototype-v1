# Channel and Entitlement Alignment

## Channel position

| Channel | Strategic position | Basis and conditions |
|---|---|---|
| **Web** (direct links, QR entry, persistent destinations, Home, Directory) | Core and independent. Every capability that a customer needs for access and discovery must work without any optional channel. | F01 §5; F03 FD-04; PAP-10. Detailed web architecture is outside F05. |
| **QR codes** | An entry route to the persistent web destination, not a dependency for later visits. | F01 §5. |
| **WhatsApp** | Optional and conditional. PEWA owns menu and order meaning and domain outcomes; the Miledge WhatsApp Adapter would own only its contracted transport boundary. | F02 FD-03; F03 FD-04; MV-207. |
| **Other channels or messaging** | Not assumed. Any would need separate evidence and authority. | F02 FD-04. |

**MWA dependency conditions preserved** (all must be independently verified before adoption; none is established by this package): current contract conformance, operational ownership, deployment and availability, support, access terms, PEWA integration readiness, and Miledge reconciliation of the MWA documentation/artifact maturity discrepancy. No Meta use, integration or provider is authorised.

**Channel strategy rules:** deterministic structured interaction first; channel events are inputs and transport delivery is not a business outcome; a channel being unavailable must not remove web access or published offerings; no PEWA parallel provider stack; no conversation design here.

## Entitlement position

| Capability | Requires | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Menu access and manual engagement (F01 mode 1) | Subscription entitlement | PEWA records no order in this mode. |
| Optional structured ordering (F01 mode 2) | Subscription entitlement **and** business enablement | Enablement cannot exceed entitlement; PEWA is accountable for entitlement meaning and capability-level enforcement. |
| Optional channels (for example WhatsApp) | Entitlement relationship undefined | Whether channel use is entitlement-governed is for Product Definition; availability also depends on MWA conditions. |
| Featured placement on PEWA Home | Separate commercial arrangement, clearly labelled | F01 §9; not a subscription feature by default; controls open. |

**Entitlement strategy rules:**

1. Entitlement determines availability, never meaning. A lower tier changes what a business may do, not what statuses or public/protected rules mean.
2. Entitlement meaning is PEWA product logic; the billing or entitlement mechanism, payment provider and any shared rail remain unresolved and evidence-gated.
3. Flat subscription without commission is the working preference to be tested, not an approved decision. Illustrative prices are not approved.
4. No plans, tiers, names, prices, limits or rollout stages are defined here.
5. **Continuity principle.** Entitlement lapse or downgrade cannot silently rewrite historical order outcomes or cause PEWA to misrepresent an order that was accepted. What was recorded as submitted, accepted or ready keeps its meaning and actor attribution regardless of later entitlement changes.

**Left open for Product Definition and Requirements (no position taken, no enforcement design proposed):** menu visibility after lapse or downgrade; completion of active orders; downgrade behaviour; grace periods; suspension policy; how a business or customer is told a capability is unavailable; whether payment during Place Order is in the first scope.

## Not decided

Providers, channel interactions, integrations, message content, subscription plans, payment handling, deployment.
