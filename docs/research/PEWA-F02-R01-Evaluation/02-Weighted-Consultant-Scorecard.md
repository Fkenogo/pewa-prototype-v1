# PEWA-F02-R01-B — Weighted Consultant Scorecard

**Status:** Comparative evidence scores for Founder review; not an approval or taxonomy decision.

## Method

Six criteria and weights are from the Founder-authorised methodology in [00-Evaluation-Scope-and-Criteria-Reconstruction.md](00-Evaluation-Scope-and-Criteria-Reconstruction.md). Each criterion receives an integer score from 0 to 5.

| Score | Meaning |
|---:|---|
| 0 | Absent/unassessable |
| 1 | Minimal |
| 2 | Partial with significant weaknesses |
| 3 | Adequate with gaps |
| 4 | Strong |
| 5 | Comprehensive and well-supported |

**Weighted total = SUM(score/5 × criterion weight).** With weights expressed as percentage points, the total is out of 100. A score rates submitted evidence, not whether a proposed class is ultimately correct. Confidence labels describe confidence in the score rationale from the submitted record; no external verification was performed.

## Interpretation limits

Scores are specific to the evidence submitted and the reconstructed A–H scope. A complete deliverable set does not imply high research quality, and record counts are not rewarded by themselves. The close Consultant 3/6 totals and the lower totals for Consultants 1/2/4/5 should guide review priorities only; they do not select a taxonomy, transfer authority, or justify merging any consultant’s classifications.
