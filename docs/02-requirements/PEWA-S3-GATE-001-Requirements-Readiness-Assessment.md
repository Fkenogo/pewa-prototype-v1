# PEWA-S3-GATE-001 — Stage 3 Requirements Readiness Assessment

**Status:** FOUNDER REVIEW CANDIDATE — NOT A CLOSURE OR STAGE 4 AUTHORISATION  
**Date:** 18 October 2026  
**Basis:** R01–R08 (311 numbered requirements), [REC-001](PEWA-S3-REC-001-Consolidated-Requirements-Reconciliation.md), approved COM-D01–D07, ORD-D01–D08, BMP-D01–D08, CPN-D01–D09 and POP-D01–D09.  
**Scope:** Determine whether Stage 3 has a coherent, reviewable product requirements baseline, and separate release/legal/commercial specifications from genuine product-definition gaps.

## 1. Assessment

**Proposed disposition: CONDITIONAL READINESS FOR STAGE 3 CLOSURE REVIEW, NOT AUTOMATIC CLOSURE.**

The approved requirements and Founder policy decisions cover business management, public/protected multilingual menus, Home and organic Directory search, manual customer engagement and structured ordering, one standard subscription with 200 monthly included acceptance units, top-ups and 10 recoverable continuity units, Featured Offers, guest protection, privacy, notifications and bounded Operator Console functions. No architectural or provider choice is implied.

Forty-one Founder policy directions have been considered across five packages (COM 7, ORD 8, BMP 8, CPN 9, POP 9). They are now approved at product-policy level. Remaining gaps fall into four categories, which must not be casually treated as decisions already made.

## 2. Closure readiness conditions

**C1 — Canonical reconciliation:** R01–R08 and the consolidated reconciliation shall explicitly preserve the approved standard-subscription model, merchant language freedom, order meanings, protection of existing commitments, organic/paid distinction, and scoped PEWA platform authority. Legacy terminology suggesting a separately purchased ordering *plan* must not accidentally override the approved 200-included-unit model.

**C2 — Technical specification is not silently approved:** Numeric defaults and detailed edge handling for pending order expiry, overlapping availability schedules, simultaneous last-unit acceptance, protected link recovery, exposure viewability/deduplication, fraud/appeals and notification delivery remain **unresolved implementation/product policy details**. Any detail that materially changes a customer's commitment, billing or privacy rights requires Founder product or competent policy approval before the relevant capability is implemented or launched.

**C3 — Commercial launch gate:** Illustrative US$10/month, US$0.05/unit and US$10 per 200 top-up units are **not final rates**. Local-currency price, taxes, billing conditions, grace duration, top-up validity, recovery settlement and Enterprise contract obligations require explicit separate approval before billing customers.

**C4 — Legal and privacy launch gate:** Product decisions must be expressed in suitable Business Terms, Customer Terms, Privacy Notice, consent wording and Platform Policies, with jurisdiction-specific competent review before live collection of personal data and binding contractual deployment. Founder approval of requirements is not legal certification.

**C5 — Platform dependency boundary:** Web first remains independent; Miledge WhatsApp Adapter use requires separately evidenced shared-service authority. No external provider or operational integration is selected.

**C6 — Stage and experience gates:** Product architecture (Stage 4), FEF adoption (Stage 5), and engineering (Stage 6) remain NOT STARTED unless expressly separately authorised. Full Operator Console experience is assembled last in approved implementation sequencing.

Do not reopen R01–R08 unless a material contradiction is demonstrated. Review that package and issue separate Stage 3 closure disposition.
