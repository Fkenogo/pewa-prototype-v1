## Stage controls

- Each stage has its own explicit Founder initiation, review, disposition and closure. No stage begins automatically, including on closure of another.
- FEF guidance may inform stages 1–4 without implying FEF adoption or engineering readiness. Final engineering readiness is a separate authority gate.
- Open decisions are allocated to stages in the [open-decision register](../research/PEWA-PHT-001/04-Future-Programme-Sequence-and-Open-Decision-Allocation.md) (26 items), a controlled planning and ownership register. Items may be reallocated through approved project governance without reopening the transition, provided no existing authority or approved decision is silently changed.
- Material changes to approved Miledge or FEF authority affecting PEWA are reconciled before the relevant dependent decision; immaterial updates do not block work.
