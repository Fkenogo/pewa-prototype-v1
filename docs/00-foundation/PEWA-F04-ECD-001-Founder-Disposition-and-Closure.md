## Founder disposition

The Founder **approved the corrected F04 Engine Capability Definition** and closed F04. The six Founder-review corrections (PEWA-F04-ECD-001-CORR-001) are part of the approved package.

## Approved decisions

1. **Capability boundary.** The eight proposed engine capability areas (A–H) are approved as PEWA's product-level capability boundary. They are not a Miledge Enduring Capability, Domain or Shared Platform classification.
2. **Operating modes.** Both are approved: (a) entitlement-governed manual customer engagement, facilitated through published offerings and business contact pathways; and (b) optional structured ordering, requiring both subscription entitlement and business enablement.
3. **PEWA accountability.** PEWA remains accountable for food-service product/domain semantics, entitlement meaning, product integrity, accurate outcome communication, handling reports of misleading or problematic content, and applicable platform consumer-protection obligations.
4. **Business authority.** Businesses retain authority over supplied offerings, prices, availability, order acceptance and readiness, and fulfilment.
5. **Unresolved exceptions.** Authority for cancellation, correction and amendment is left unresolved for later product definition and requirements.
6. **Data responsibility.** The six-class data responsibility inventory is accepted at capability level only. It establishes no legal ownership, canonical technical data authority, retention rules or storage design.
7. **Reuse hypothesis.** The sector-neutral reusable capability hypothesis is retained without expanding the approved F01 scope.
8. **Dependencies.** MWA adoption remains conditional, web access remains independent, and shared-service dependencies remain evidence-gated.

## Boundary of this closure

This disposition does not amend canonical Miledge architecture, admit PEWA, assign portfolio classification, approve an Enduring Capability, Domain, Shared Platform or Shared Service ownership, select providers, specify technical modules, or authorise implementation. Approved F01–F03 records are unchanged.

**F05 — Product Configuration & Experience Strategy is next eligible and has not started.** It requires its own explicit start authority.

## Unresolved dependencies carried forward

- Canonical technical authority for menu and order records; tenancy mechanism (F03 FD-01, FD-02).
- Cancellation, correction and amendment authority; payment and acceptance safeguards.
- Customer-information authority and per-class lifecycle, privacy, retention, export, correction and deletion decisions.
- Reporting, moderation, enforcement and appeals processes.
- Billing, payment provider, tier and entitlement implementation.
- MWA operational readiness and PEWA integration; every other shared-service candidate.
