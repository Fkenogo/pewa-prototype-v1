## Regional and research gaps

1. **Burundi menu structure and language validation:** C3 and C6 explicitly say available Burundi evidence is thin or full sit-down menu structure absent. C4 has a regional category set but acknowledges no primary menu observation. C5 claims Bujumbura fieldwork but supplies no audit trail. Kirundi/Kinyarwanda/Luganda/Lingala synonyms remain unvalidated.
2. **Central, Horn, North, Southern and broader West Africa:** Coverage exists in some catalogues but is uneven, often based on dish inventories rather than observed menu structures. C3 and C6 state gaps; C5 and C4 make broader claims with weak traceability.
3. **Specialist operators:** Tea houses, juice bars, confectionery, institutional foodservice and local beverages have unequal or sparse evidence.
4. **Search behavior:** No submission provides a customer study in PEWA priority markets that quantifies query vocabulary, synonym acceptance or false-positive costs.
5. **Cross-consultant source quality:** Consultants 1, 2 and 5 do not provide an auditable source key; Consultant 4’s citations are often named-only; Consultants 3 and 6 have source registers but those sources have not been checked.
