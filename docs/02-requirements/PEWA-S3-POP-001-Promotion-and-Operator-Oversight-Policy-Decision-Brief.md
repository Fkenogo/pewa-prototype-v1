# PEWA-S3-POP-001 — Promotion and Operator Oversight Policy Decision Brief

**Status:** FOUNDER APPROVED — POP-D01–D09, 18 October 2026, PR #37. Numerical thresholds and legal details remain open.  
**Date:** 18 October 2026  
**Authority:** PEWA Stage 2 Product Definition; approved R03, R05, R06, R07, R08; approved COM, ORD, BMP and CPN directions.  
**Scope:** Business/commercial and platform-integrity product policy, not a fixed price, advert serving architecture, enforcement law, provider selection or deployment approval.

## Boundaries not reopened

Featured Offers are separately purchased and visibly sponsored, without buying organic Directory rank. An eligible on-screen **opportunity to see**, not an off-screen load or guaranteed sale, grounds delivered impression accounting. Own-menu business specials need no paid campaign. Consumer food payments, food preparation, order acceptance/Ready and fulfilment stay with businesses. PEWA operators oversee platform integrity, business eligibility, billing corrections, reports and privacy with role-scoped attributed actions, rather than acting as merchants.

## Founder-approved directions

### POP-D01 — Qualifying impressions
**Approved direction:** Count a purchased Featured Offer impression only if the specific paid placement is rendered within a customer-visible on-screen area under a defined viewability threshold and a genuine opportunity to see. Exclude merely scheduled, prefetched, off-screen or obscured placement, and invalid/nonhuman traffic.
- **Open:** viewable area/time threshold, repeated exposure deduplication, invalid-traffic evidence and technical measurement; no arbitrary numbers selected.
- **Acceptance:** a hidden tile below the unscrolled Home page cannot automatically consume exposure.

### POP-D02 — Campaign inventory, delivery and reports
**Approved direction:** Promotions start with eligible, clearly marked PEWA Home placements. New discovery surfaces require separately approved treatment, with organic ranking always independent. A business sees purchased, eligible delivered and remaining impressions; optional click/visit measures are not represented as conversions.
- **Open:** available surfaces, allocation fairness/caps, viewability validity and capacity/oversubscription safeguards.
- **Acceptance:** 20 delivered qualifying views out of 100 purchased report 20 delivered, not 100.

### POP-D03 — Self-service and content responsibility
**Approved direction:** Authorised merchants prepare, purchase, pause and review campaigns using their own eligible public published content, within product safeguards; routine PEWA staff allocation or copy rewriting is not required. False or materially inconsistent business claims may trigger bounded restriction/appeal.
- **Acceptance:** a hotel can promote a public restaurant menu but not its protected room-service menu.

### POP-D04 — Undelivered exposure and interruption
**Approved direction:** On merchant pause, subscription suspension, enforcement or delivery interruption, preserve evidence of actual valid impressions and distinguish undelivered purchased entitlement. Suspend future delivery where ineligible; resolve remaining exposure under clearly disclosed renewal, extension, expiry or credit/refund policy, not automatic billing for scheduled displays.
- **Open:** precise expiry, restart and compensation/transfer terms; legal rights as applicable.
- **Acceptance:** hidden/unserved ads do not consume the purchased balance merely because the campaign was scheduled.

### POP-D05 — Customer privacy and targeting
**Approved direction:** Allow contextual eligibility (public menu category and business-declared geography/time) without mandatory location access. Do not deploy individual cross-business tracking, personal retargeting or purchase of identifiable recents/favourites. Any later behavioural targeting requires separate product/privacy authority.
- **Acceptance:** a bakery sees aggregate delivery, not a list of named visitors to competing venues.

### POP-D06 — Platform operator role segregation
**Approved direction:** Define proportionate separate operator permissions for business eligibility, customer/privacy support, commercial billing corrections, campaign integrity and highly sensitive administrator functions. No ordinary operator gets automatic unrestricted customer/order access or merchant order-management rights.
- **Open:** detailed responsibility matrix, exceptional-access review thresholds, privileged recovery.
- **Acceptance:** an advertising reviewer cannot mark a restaurant's order Ready or read unrelated room details.

### POP-D07 — Attributable consequential action and appeals
**Approved direction:** Material platform decisions—business restriction, campaign pause, customer-data access, corrected consumption/impressions and permission changes—require authorised actor, reason and reviewable history. Provide notice and a route to contest appropriate adverse decisions, allowing emergency restrictions only under proportionate policy.
- **Open:** proof standards, deadlines, independence of review, emergency exceptions and remedies.
- **Acceptance:** mistakenly restricted genuine business can see a review pathway without PEWA silently rewriting its menu.

### POP-D08 — Honest oversight reporting
**Approved direction:** Separate menu views, qualifying campaign exposure, contact actions, submitted and business-accepted orders, recoverable units and PEWA commercial charges. Do not present merchant cash sales, meal payments, fulfilment, food quality or physical delivery as PEWA-verified metrics.
- **Acceptance:** operator dashboard never infers restaurant revenue from accepted-order count.

### POP-D09 — Bounded support and consumer complaints
**Approved direction:** PEWA accepts platform-related fraud, misleading listings, exposure/billing disputes and privacy reports, routes them to authorised scoped staff and records disposition; merchant-dependent food quality, payment/refund and actual preparation disputes remain merchant responsibilities subject to law and appropriate platform integrity intervention.
- **Acceptance:** PEWA can investigate impersonation but cannot claim to have independently delivered a meal.

## Terms and policy handover

Business subscription and campaign terms must describe sponsorship labelling, validity of exposure, eligible placements, pauses, expiry/credit, the business's truthfulness duties and billing dispute pathways. Customer terms and privacy notice explain paid placement, PEWA's intermediary role, data use and complaints. Operator policies define privileges, correction, incidents, proportional enforcement and appeals. Applicable legal requirements and exact prices require competent separate review.

## Review and stage boundaries

These directions feed into future business/customer Terms and Conditions, privacy notices and operator policies, not automatically enforceable published terms.
