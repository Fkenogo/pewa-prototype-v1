# PEWA-S3-CPN-001 — Guest Access, Privacy and Notifications Policy Decision Brief

**Status:** FOUNDER APPROVED — CPN-D01–D09, 18 October 2026, PR #35; detailed legal and verification policy remains open  
**Date:** 18 October 2026  
**Authority:** Approved PEWA Constitution and Stage 2 Product Definition, R03 Customer Experience, R04 Order Lifecycle, R07 Customer Protection, R08 Operator Console, approved ORD-D01–D08 and BMP-D01–D08.  
**Boundary:** Product-policy decision proposals only. No identity-provider, authentication architecture, legal opinion, data-retention duration, notification implementation or Stage 4 authorisation.

## Settled constraints

Public menus and Directory search require no customer account, location permission, push or PWA installation. Hotels can restrict selected guest menus while keeping genuinely public dining menus public. A hotel guest is a normal PEWA customer, not a special compulsory customer-account class. Account-free customers need protected order-status return. Web/in-app operational records are primary; push is optional; automatic outbound WhatsApp order-status messaging is disabled. Marketing permissions, local favourites and browsing privacy are distinct from operational access. Businesses cannot see cross-business browsing histories or unrelated private orders.

## Founder-approved policy directions

### CPN-D01 — Protected menu access is context-scoped and time-bounded
**Approved direction:** A hotel or authorised business configures the audience for each restricted menu/service context. Protected access requires eligibility appropriate to the context, expires/revokes under a disclosed policy, and must not expose guest or room information through public search or Featured Offers. A restricted-menu link alone should not confer unlimited indefinite authorisation.
- **Alternative:** Require full PEWA customer accounts for every hotel guest — not recommended, inconsistent with guest-first strategy.
- **Still open:** hotel proof method, group/room-share delegation, visit length, revocation and emergency exceptions.
- **Acceptance:** a former guest cannot browse a protected room menu solely using an old link; the hotel's public restaurant remains searchable.

### CPN-D02 — Protected order access without universal registration
**Approved direction:** Each valid guest order has a protected return capability under proportionate proof of possession/control, separated from a publicly guessable order reference. Account enrolment, device push and PWA install are never mandatory for status retrieval.
- **Still open:** exact assurance method and safe expiration/recovery.
- **Acceptance:** the legitimate customer revisits order status but another person who knows only an order number cannot inspect it.

### CPN-D03 — Stronger authorisation for order-changing actions
**Approved direction:** Apply stronger proportional checks for withdrawal, cancellation, amendment approval, sensitive information changes and protected account-linking than for reading limited permitted status. Do not allow an actor with only a public order reference to make commercially consequential changes.
- **Acceptance:** a customer can read legitimate Pending status without gaining the ability to cancel another person's accepted order.

### CPN-D04 — Safe recovery and optional customer identity
**Approved direction:** Provide a bounded recovery path when the original guest loses authorised order access. If a customer voluntarily registers, cross-device order/favourite history linking requires adequate ownership proof, and creating an account alone grants no historical access. Prioritise minimal data collection.
- **Still open:** acceptable proof, support escalation, abusive attempts, expiry, account recovery.
- **Acceptance:** merely registering with a matching display name cannot import another customer's orders.

### CPN-D05 — Web notification record is authoritative customer experience
**Approved direction:** PEWA's protected web/in-app record carries meaningful order updates: Submitted, Accepted, Declined, Expired, Withdrawn, Ready (business-declared), cancellation requests/outcomes, amendments. An external delivery acknowledgment is never treated as merchant acceptance.
- **Acceptance:** customer refuses all device notifications but can still check an accurately attributed business declaration.

### CPN-D06 — Optional push and no default outbound WhatsApp
**Approved direction:** Offer push only when product/device supports it and customer opts in, with revocation and privacy-safe previews. Declining push cannot block ordering or trigger fallback outbound WhatsApp messages. Future WhatsApp transport remains contingent on separate Miledge approval and product/channel consent.
- **Acceptance:** denying push still permits web status access and causes no unsolicited WhatsApp updates.

### CPN-D07 — Marketing consent is independent
**Approved direction:** Treat marketing/promotion permission, location consent, optional account sync, push and PWA installation as separate user decisions. Organic recents/favourites must not become merchant-visible targeting lists. Behaviour-based retargeting is not authorised as an implicit feature.
- **Acceptance:** adding PEWA to the home screen does not opt the customer into Featured Offer messaging or personalised advertisement tracking.

### CPN-D08 — Purpose-limited data and lawful records
**Approved direction:** Distinguish intentionally public business content from protected guest information, customer contact, order history, preferences, business billing and operator audit records. Gather and retain the least necessary information for an identified purpose, with correction/deletion/export where applicable; preserve legitimate order/usage evidence to the extent legally authorised.
- **Still open:** per-country lawful bases, notices, retention windows, minor/age policies, cross-border duties, incident deadlines and deletion exceptions; competent privacy/legal review required.
- **Acceptance:** removal of a local favourite does not erase a lawful accepted-order usage record, and a campaign buyer cannot inspect identifiable customer browsing history.

### CPN-D09 — Complaints, misuse and privacy escalation
**Approved direction:** Establish accessible customer/business pathways to report protected-access misuse, misleading publication, food claims and privacy exposure. Operator investigation is role-limited, attributable, proportionate and subject to appropriate notices/appeals.
- **Still open:** statutory timelines, escalation levels, rights-response periods and remedies.
- **Acceptance:** hotel room-data exposure is referred to authorised PEWA staff without sharing unrelated guest or merchant details.

## Platform-policy and Terms handover

- **Customer-facing terms:** guest menu eligibility, order access/recovery limitations, customer action authority and truthful notifications.
- **Business terms:** responsibility for restricting guest audiences, protected data, staff access, accuracy, and cancellation/communication obligations.
- **Privacy notice/consents:** purposes, account/local preferences, push/marketing separation, retention, correction/deletion rights and permitted sharing.
- **Operator policy:** bounded privileged case access, breach escalation, evidentiary records, privacy complaints and appeals.
- Competent jurisdictional legal review is required before enforceable legal publication; this package does not select a provider or certify compliance.
