# E. Founder Transition Decision Brief

## Recommendation: AUTHORISE WITH CONDITIONS

PEWA meets the Founder-approved transition gate. All five foundation stages are closed, F05 Conditions A–C are carried forward, unresolved questions have named later stages, the six-stage sequence is authoritative, and authority boundaries with Miledge and FEF are intact. **No genuine blocker was found** ([document 1](01-Transition-Readiness-Assessment.md)).

Authorising the transition establishes the PEWA repository as the product-home programme and its authority boundary. It does **not** start any stage, authorise any FEF adoption, admit or classify PEWA, or approve engineering.

## Proposed conditions

1. **Interpretation of the Miledge baseline.** Confirm that the gate's "approved Miledge baseline/position" is satisfied by the F02 prospective, hypothesis-only position and the inspected Miledge and FEF baselines, and that Miledge admission/classification remains unresolved with Miledge authority. (If not, the outcome becomes HOLD pending a Miledge disposition.)
2. **Programme control record.** On disposition, record a project-home programme control (extend the Foundation Programme or add a project-home programme record) carrying the six-stage sequence and this allocation, and update the README status. Documentation only.
3. **No stage starts automatically.** Stage 1 (Constitution) and each later stage requires its own explicit Founder start instruction and independent closure.
4. **Re-confirm on baseline change.** If Miledge or FEF publish a new approved baseline materially affecting the carried-forward positions before Stage 1 starts, re-confirm the affected position.

## Alternatives

- **HOLD** if the Founder reads the gate as requiring an actual Miledge admission/classification, or wants the allocation reworked first.
- **RETURN FOR CORRECTION** if any allocation or authority statement is wrong.
- **AUTHORISE (no conditions)** is possible but leaves the programme-control record and interpretation unrecorded.

## Remains unresolved after transition

Miledge classification questions; canonical data authority and tenancy; subscriptions and entitlement lapse policy; order exceptions; customer-data authority and lifecycle; consumer protection and moderation; hotel restricted access; shared services and MWA readiness; FEF alignment. All are allocated in document 4.

## Proposed decision statement

Subject to the Founder's disposition, PEWA is authorised to move from its completed Miledge-level foundation programme to its dedicated product-home programme under the stated authority boundary. The approved six-stage sequence governs, each stage requires its own authority, Miledge retains portfolio and shared-infrastructure authority, FEF adoption and engineering remain separately gated, and no stage, adoption or implementation begins by this decision.
