## Founder disposition

The Founder **approved PAP-01 through PAP-12 as PEWA foundation-level architectural principles, subject to the six conditions below**, and closed F03. The principles remain subject to their source traceability in the supporting [principles proposal](../research/PEWA-F03-PAP-001/01-Proposed-Platform-Architecture-Principles.md), with PAP-03 clarified to protect authorized public menu publication and discovery while preventing unauthorized disclosure of protected organisation data.

The Founder authorized F03 to proceed to substantive review on 16 October 2026. This records the start authority that preceded this disposition. F04 is next eligible and has not started.

## Six approval conditions

### FD-01 — Business authority and canonical technical state

Participating businesses retain authority over the meaning and correctness of supplied offerings, prices, availability and business decisions. PEWA remains responsible for truthful validation, presentation and product behavior it performs. **Canonical technical authority for menu records and order state remains unresolved**, including write/publish responsibility.

### FD-02 — Secure organisation isolation; tenancy mechanism deferred

Secure organisation boundaries, least privilege and product-domain authorization are mandatory. Prevent unauthorized access and disclosure of protected organisation data. Public offerings deliberately published by a business may be discovered across organisation boundaries through approved product controls. **The tenancy implementation is deferred**; no tenant model, identity provider or role matrix is selected.

### FD-03 — Reuse hypothesis and approved initial context

Sector-neutral structured-offerings publishing/access/discovery and optional structured selection remain a **working hypothesis**. Food, beverages and meal-related offerings remain PEWA's approved initial context under F01. The hypothesis does not broaden scope into general commerce or approve a Miledge Enduring Capability, Domain or Shared Platform position.

### FD-04 — Conditional WhatsApp; independent web access

The Miledge WhatsApp Adapter remains a **conditional shared dependency**, subject to current contract conformance, operational ownership, availability, support, access and PEWA integration readiness being independently verified. **Independent web access is required** and must remain available when an optional channel or shared service is unavailable. No integration is authorized by this disposition.

### FD-05 — Infrastructure and provider authority

Architecture-driven infrastructure assessment and proportionate provider neutrality apply. **Provider selection, infrastructure design and deployment are not authorized** by F03. No database, hosting, provider, deployment topology or technical implementation is selected.

### FD-06 — Later mandatory decision topics

Privacy, data export/correction/deletion/retention and portability, auditability, failure isolation, recovery and operational responsibility remain mandatory topics for subsequent decisions. Their detailed policies, periods, targets, designs and owners require the relevant later authority and evidence.

## F03 scope and Miledge authority boundary

This disposition accepts the principles as PEWA foundation-level constraints. It does not make or imply a Miledge decision under MPA-002/MPA-005. It does **not** admit PEWA, assign portfolio classification, approve an Enduring Capability, Domain, Shared Platform, Shared Service ownership or layer mapping, or change canonical Miledge architecture. Draft Miledge records remain non-binding.

No F04/F05 activity, detailed Product Architecture, application implementation or engineering is authorized by this closure. Later stages remain governed by the approved Foundation Programme and separate authority gates.
