## 2. Founder-approved closure disposition

By explicit Founder approval of PR #40 on 18 October 2026:

1. **Approve closure of Stage 3 — Requirements Development and Approval**, on the basis of R01–R08 and the policy decisions above as PEWA's authoritative product-requirements baseline.
2. **Accept with explicit reserved conditions**, rather than representing prices, consumer-law compliance, identity design, measured impression thresholds or implementation readiness as complete.
3. **Preserve PEWA authority boundaries.** PEWA publishes business-authored menus and provides optional structured requests, but does not become the food merchant, order-payment intermediary, meal fulfiller or logistics service; business acceptance/Ready remains business-declared.
4. **Retain the unified commercial model:** one organisation standard monthly listing subscription with 200 included acceptance units whether ordering is used or not; separate purchased top-up bundles; bounded 10 recoverable units as debt-backed continuity; negotiable Enterprise arrangements. Numerical US dollar examples remain illustrative and not commercially binding.
5. **Retain language and discovery flexibility:** PEWA navigation English first; business-authored menu names, descriptions and categories in any language; optional shared classification for search, not mandatory taxonomy or unreviewed food/allergen/translation claim.
6. **Retain customer protections and promotion integrity:** direct independent public web, appropriately protected hotel guest menus and account-free order access, optional push/no automatic outbound WhatsApp status, paid Featured Offers disclosed and charged on qualifying on-screen exposure, not purchased organic rank or guaranteed sales.
7. **Carry outstanding duties into governed future decisions and Terms/Privacy handover**; material changes to settled requirements return for Founder product disposition, and no architecture work assumes unspecified operating policies are approved.
8. **No automatic start of Stage 4, Stage 5 or Stage 6.** Product Architecture must receive its own Founder instruction; portfolio/shared-service and FEF/engineering gates remain separate.
