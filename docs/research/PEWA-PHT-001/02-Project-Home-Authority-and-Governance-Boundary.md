# B. Project-Home Authority and Governance Boundary

Proposed distinctions for the project home. They restate approved authority and add no new classification of PEWA within Miledge.

## Authority map

| Authority | Decides | Does not decide | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Founder** | Stage start, disposition and closure for every project-home stage; scope changes; the transition itself; engineering authorisation; Founder-level commercial direction. | — (final authority within Miledge and FEF rules) | Foundation Programme; FEF Adoption Guide §§3, 5 |
| **PEWA product authority** (Founder acting for the product, through the PEWA repository) | Product governance (Constitution), Product Definition, requirements, product-level domain meaning (food-service semantics, entitlement meaning, status meanings), product accountability positions, product-level technical decisions *within* Miledge and FEF boundaries. | Miledge admission, classification, Domains, Shared Platforms, shared-service ownership or contracts; FEF baseline content. | F02 FD-02; F04 decisions 1–4; F05 D1–D4 |
| **Miledge portfolio authority** (MPA-002/MPA-005, MTAIP-001, owners of shared services) | PEWA admission and classification (if any), Enduring Capability/Domain/Shared Platform decisions, layer mapping, shared-service existence, ownership, contract, maturity and availability (including MWA conformance and operations). | PEWA product-domain meaning or business facts. | F02 FD-04/FD-05; F03 FD-03/FD-04; F04 decision 8 |

| **External providers** (for example messaging, hosting, payment, if ever selected) | Their own operated service under their own agreements. | Product meaning or business outcomes; none is selected. | F03 FD-05; F04 provider boundary |

## Rules for the project home

1. The PEWA repository is the project's source of truth for its own state; Miledge documents are cited by version, not copied into authority.
2. Where a project-home decision implicates a Miledge matter (shared service, layer, classification), it is referred to Miledge authority and recorded as a dependency, not decided locally.
3. Each of the six stages is independently governed: its own start authority, review, Founder disposition and closure.
4. FEF standards may inform earlier stages (for example evidence and review practice) without implying FEF adoption or engineering readiness; engineering readiness is step 5/6 and separately gated.
5. Nothing in the project home may reopen F01–F05 without an explicit Founder instruction.
