# Project-Home Transition Readiness and Handover

## Boundary

F05 is the last Miledge-level foundation stage. This document proposes what to hand over. **Closing F05 does not authorise the transition.** The transition requires a separate Founder disposition confirming the approved Miledge baseline, the handover of unresolved items and project authority.

## Currently approved sequence

Product Governance Foundation/Constitution → Product Definition → Requirements → FEF adoption/readiness → separately authorised engineering.

This is the sequence in force. Each step needs its own authority, and nothing in this package starts any of them.

## Proposed Founder amendment to the sequence (not yet effective)

F03 and F04 allocate several technical decisions (canonical data authority, tenancy enforcement, shared-service dependency choice, recovery and operations) to "later Product Architecture", but the currently approved sequence has no explicit Product Architecture step. **The Founder is asked to amend the sequence** so the project-home programme runs:

1. Product Governance Foundation / Constitution.
2. Product Definition.
3. Requirements development and approval.
4. Product Architecture and technical decisions.
5. FEF adoption/readiness confirmation.
6. Separately authorised engineering.

This amendment is a **proposal only**. It takes effect only if the Founder approves it in the F05 disposition (or a later instruction); until then the currently approved sequence above governs, and the Foundation Programme is not changed by this package. Approved F01–F04 dispositions are unaffected, and no Product Architecture work begins.

**FEF relationship.** FEF standards may inform earlier stages (for example governance structure, evidence and review practice in the Constitution, Product Definition and Requirements). Under the proposal, FEF adoption/readiness *confirmation* sits after Product Architecture, and final engineering readiness remains a separate gate with its own authority. Informing earlier stages is not FEF engineering readiness.

## Pre-transition prerequisites versus matters decided inside the project home

**Prerequisites for the transition disposition** (the Founder needs these in order to authorise the move; kept deliberately small):

- F05 dispositioned and closed.
- Handover register accepted as the input list, with each unresolved item having a named later stage.
- The Founder's decision on the proposed sequence amendment.
- Confirmation of the approved Miledge baseline and project authority for the project home.

**Decided inside the project home** (carried forward, *not* automatic pre-transition blockers): MWA readiness and adoption; entitlement lapse, downgrade, grace and suspension behaviour; cancellation, correction and amendment authority; customer-data authority and per-class lifecycle; hotel guest-only or other restricted access; and the other items in the register below. Each is assigned to the stage where it is naturally decided (Constitution, Product Definition, Requirements or Product Architecture).

## Handover register

### 1. Approved foundation decisions (carry forward unchanged)

F01 thesis and exclusions; F02 FD-01–FD-05; F03 PAP-01–PAP-12 and FD-01–FD-06; F04 eight areas, both modes, accountability split, six-class data inventory (capability level only), cancellation/correction/amendment left open; and, if approved, the F05 strategy.

### 2. Product Governance / Constitution questions

- Product principles and decision authority inside PEWA (who decides what, escalation to Founder and to Miledge).
- How PEWA records product truth versus proposals, and how F01–F05 become project-home source of truth.
- Standards for consumer-protection posture and accountability statements.
- Relationship of project governance to FEF and to Miledge architecture authority.

### 3. Product Definition decisions

Required fields and readiness-to-publish; multi-menu organisation semantics; guest/account model and recents/favourites; discovery, search, location and ranking; featured placement controls; approved contact-pathway set; permitted service contexts; availability handling; configuration subjects and who may change them; entitlement-lapse behaviour; customer-facing experience of both modes.

### 4. Requirements needing research or Founder disposition

Market validation of F01 hypotheses; subscription tiers, prices, limits and rollout; payment during Place Order and payment/acceptance safeguards; cancellation, correction and amendment authority; hotel guest-only access; reporting, moderation, enforcement and appeals; advertising controls; any sector expansion.

### 5. Privacy, consumer protection and data lifecycle

Per-class (business/organisation, offering, customer, selection/order, media, operational/audit/telemetry) lifecycle, privacy basis, retention, export, correction, deletion and portability; customer-data authority and controller roles; telemetry and audit scope; jurisdiction-specific consumer-protection and legal review; media rights and takedown.

### 6. Canonical data authority, tenancy and technical architecture

Canonical technical authority for menu and order records and write/publish responsibility; tenancy mechanism and organisation isolation; identity and authorization approach; operational recovery and support responsibility; these belong to the proposed Product Architecture and technical-decisions stage. They stay unresolved and are not decided by F05.

### 7. Shared infrastructure and MWA

MWA adoption conditions (conformance, ownership, availability, support, access, PEWA readiness) and Miledge's reconciliation of the documentation/artifact maturity discrepancy; media, identity, tenancy, search, subscriptions, billing, analytics, audit and notifications as evidence-gated candidates; any Miledge admission, classification or layer mapping remains with Miledge authority.

### 8. FEF adoption and readiness prerequisites

FEF standards may inform earlier project-home stages; final engineering readiness is a separate gate. FEF Adoption Guide v1.0 (approved, active) is proportionate and does not replace PEWA's own project authority. Prerequisites to confirm at transition: a controlled FEF alignment record in the project's governance structure; identified project programme source of truth; Founder review of adoption actions; gaps, deviations and conflicts surfaced for decision rather than silently resolved; and separate authority before any engineering. The Product Governance Foundation/Constitution should exist before FEF readiness is judged; under the proposed amendment, readiness confirmation follows Product Architecture.
