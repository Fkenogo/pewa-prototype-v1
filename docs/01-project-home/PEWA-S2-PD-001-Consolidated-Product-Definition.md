## 1. Identity and proposition

PEWA is a food-service menu publishing, discovery, persistent-access and customer-engagement product. Its central proposition is accessible, accurate-as-presented business-controlled menus that customers can discover and revisit. Structured ordering and paid Featured Offers are optional additional value, not prerequisites for a worthwhile menu-presence subscription.

Approved contexts: restaurants, cafés, bakeries, confectioneries, hotels (restaurants, bars, breakfast and room service), and comparable food/beverage businesses, including delivery-only businesses. Cross-industry structured-offerings reuse remains a hypothesis, not authorised market expansion.

PEWA is **not** the food merchant, general-commerce marketplace, cross-business checkout, POS, stock/inventory system, kitchen controller, courier/dispatch operator, physical fulfilment verifier, or ordinary commercial-dispute adjudicator. No food-order payments, escrow, settlement or refunds through PEWA. Business payments to PEWA for its services are distinct and permitted.

## 2. Actors, responsibility and operating modes

- **Participating organisation:** accountable subscriber and billing party, managing legitimately related brands, locations and menus.
- **Public business brand:** identifiable customer-facing provider, distinct where appropriate from the registered organisation.
- **Customer:** discovers menus, optionally saves preferences and, where offered, submits an order request.
- **Authorised business staff:** act only within granted responsibility, brand, location and capability scopes.
- **PEWA operator:** administers platform participation, entitlements, abuse, support and commercial integrity under bounded authority.

Businesses control commercial accuracy of descriptions, images, prices, declared availability and service coverage; acceptance, declination, readiness, customer commercial relationship, preparation and fulfilment. PEWA faithfully presents declarations and handles its own product integrity, event recording, access/privacy, communications, billing/exposure accounting and applicable legal/platform obligations. Business autonomy does not prevent proportionate action against fraudulent, harmful or misleading content.

Two modes coexist:
1. **Menu visibility/manual engagement:** browse and use business-controlled contact paths, including externally; no PEWA order/cart or structured order record is presumed.
2. **Optional structured PEWA ordering:** requires both business subscription entitlement and explicit enablement. Provides selection, business-declared statuses and protected order history; not physical fulfilment.

## 3. Business identity, onboarding and workspace

A single guided, resumable, low-friction onboarding accommodates single-location micro-cafés, hotels, multi-brand groups and delivery-only organisations. Separate account creation, publication eligibility and subscription activation, without forcing one long registration. Collect only necessary accountable identity and truthful publication essentials; later improvements through permanent Business Settings. Businesses can prepare unpublished content before going live.

Model distinctions: registered/admin address (not automatically public), optional customer-facing venue, declared delivery/service area, independently configurable service context and publicly recognisable brand. A delivery service can exist without a public storefront. Multiple legitimately related brands, locations, menus and team members may sit under one organisation; no arbitrary menu count cap. Publication eligibility and anti-impersonation rules prevent unrelated or fabricated brands from abusing one subscription.

Business Workspace encompasses profile and Settings; brands, venues, service areas; menus/offers; ordering when entitled; campaigns; subscription/usage; role-scoped staff; PEWA-observed reporting and notification preferences. Routine changes self-service, sensitive ownership/billing/identity changes subject to confirmation or exceptional review. An authorised team member's access may be removed without erasing the history of past actions.

## 4. Menu, offering and publishing behaviour

Businesses create several menus and categories, reuse offerings intentionally, and publish menu-level public/protected/unpublished visibility. The identity of the supplying business stays prominent on each business menu. The same descriptive offering can be shared while context-specific prices, currency, visibility and availability remain explicitly controlled; no silent propagation of unintended changes.

A compound dish (e.g. Big Breakfast) is one sellable offering, not an inventory recipe. Descriptions, images, categories, tags and offering identity are core publishing capabilities. Structured options, modifiers, required/optional choices, extras, quantities and computed selection totals apply only to entitled ordering.

Availability is **business-declared**, with location/menu/service context, recurring time periods, date windows, seasonal rules, immediate availability adjustments and bounded manual overrides. An unavailable item can stay visibly marked unavailable or be hidden at business choice. Future requested-service-time eligibility follows configured declarations, never an independently verified supply guarantee. Substantive descriptive edits use reviewable draft/publish semantics; quick availability updates need not wait for full republication. Hide/archive is reversible and does not falsify past orders.

A published public menu is independently reachable at a persistent web destination. QR and web links provide entry; WhatsApp is optional, not an underlying web-access dependency.

## 5. Prices and currency

Businesses select supported currency per relevant menu/pricing context. Different contexts can have different prices/currencies, but an individual structured order has **one explicit currency**, coherent item/extra pricing and disclosed total before submission; PEWA must not silently combine different currencies. Material post-submission price changes require proposed amendment and affected-party acknowledgement. History retains original currency and submitted amounts.

PEWA charges and subscription offers display primarily in relevant **local currency**, with USD optional rather than default. The $10/month basic-entry ceiling and ~$0.05/accepted-order working hypothesis are commercial references, **not approved local prices, conversion rules, taxes or final rates**.

## 6. Customer journeys, PEWA Home, PWA and Directory

Public menu browsing needs no registration or installation. Direct links/QR open the particular business menu rather than forcing PEWA Home first. PEWA Home is the return destination: recently visited menus, locally saved favourites, clearly labelled **paid Featured Offers**, access to search/Directory, notifications and permitted order history. Users can remove/clear recents and favourites. Optional customer identity may synchronise preferences across devices. Do not disclose browsing/favourites across businesses.

Contextual, gentle, dismissible PWA-install invitations highlight quick return, favourites and offers. Installation, account identity, permission for web push and promotional-marketing consent remain separate, optional choices.

The distinct PEWA Directory is search-first, supporting business names, public menu names, every eligible public published offering, categories, tags, location and service-area filters, plus dine-in/takeaway/delivery service modes. Customer can choose an area or optionally grant location permission. Delivery-only businesses are discoverable without revealing private preparation/registered addresses. Only intentionally public, eligible, published data is indexed; protected, unpublished, hidden or archived content stays excluded. Organic relevance is independent of paid prominence; sponsored opportunities are clearly labelled.

## 7. Hotel guests and protected access — Founder refinement

A **hotel guest is a customer**, not a separate PEWA operating class with a compulsory PEWA-specific account or exceptional merchant role. The **hotel determines and configures** which menus a guest may access, what offerings may be ordered, and the available ordering or manual-engagement modes, within PEWA product, subscription and privacy constraints.

Public hotel restaurant menus can remain public while designated guest menus may be restricted. PEWA must faithfully enforce the business-selected intended audience and order eligibility; the business must manage its menus and guest-facing configuration. Protected access must not turn into public Directory indexing or exposure of room/guest information. **Exact guest eligibility and protected-access verification rules remain to be elaborated without imposing a universal guest identity model.**

## 8. Ordering and exceptions

Customer reviews selections, quantities, options, relevant service context, one-currency prices and total before explicitly **submitting a request**, which is not business acceptance. The business must explicitly accept or decline; silence is not acceptance. Unanswered requests have communicated pending/expiry treatment, not automatic acceptance.

Primary states: **Submitted / Awaiting business decision → Accepted → business-declared Ready (if reported)**. Ready means no PEWA verification of handover, collection, transport or fulfilment. A legitimate recorded order may have **Accepted** as its latest reported primary milestone. Acceptance is the commercial usage event regardless of whether a later Ready or cancellation is reported.

Before acceptance the customer may directly withdraw an unanswered request. After acceptance a cancellation is requested and subject to business decision. Material amendment proposals (items, prices, quantity, options) need affected-party acknowledgement, preserving original history. Distinguish Declined, Withdrawn, Expired, Cancellation Requested, Cancelled, Amendment Proposed/Agreed and Ready as applicable; do not infer business facts. PEWA records, secures and communicates the permitted declarations and outcomes, but does not manage preparation, delivery or food-order payments.

A protected **guest order-status** view must be accessible without mandatory customer registration within its permitted availability window; sensitive amendment/cancellation or recovery requires appropriate authorisation. A reference alone does not grant unrestricted read/write access. Optional account linkage and long-term history require appropriate verification and retention policy.

## 9. Notifications and WhatsApp

PEWA's own protected in-app notification inbox is the primary message record. Optional web push may flash on a supported device with explicit permission and lead to the appropriate order/engagement view; no customer must enable push or install the PWA to order or check status. Operational order notifications and promotional notifications/consent are distinct.

WhatsApp is primarily a **customer-initiated menu-access and structured conversational-selection channel**, not the default sender of outbound status updates. Outbound WhatsApp notifications remain **disabled** unless a separate justified decision approves them. There is no automatic WhatsApp fallback when push is absent. PEWA web access remains independent. The intended Miledge WhatsApp Adapter is conditional, not assumed operational or technically adopted here.

## 10. Organisation subscriptions and lapse

One affordable **organisation-level base subscription** provides legitimate menu publication, Directory discoverability, web access, business-menu promotions, manual engagement and relevant management. Basic entry is aimed at micro-businesses and not above a **US$10/month reference ceiling**, subject to later locally denominated fees. Multiple menus/brands/locations are not automatically charged as additional base subscriptions; legitimacy rules apply.

Commercial states distinguish preparation, active, renewal due, bounded grace, suspended, reactivated and account closure. Lapse after grace affects **public menu visibility and subscription-dependent new engagement**, not ownership of existing content/history. Public links can show neutral unavailability, with no misleading redirection. Workspace, correction, payment/reactivation, content and historical records remain appropriately accessible and retained, subject to lawful retention/erasure. Payment restoration can revive eligible published content without re-onboarding; payment never overrides an independent integrity restriction. Closure and misconduct enforcement are different from non-payment.

## 11. Ordering consumption and bounded continuity — Founder refinement

Ordering is optional and usage-driven. **One business acceptance triggers one chargeable accepted-order consumption event**, with identical meaning regardless of web or WhatsApp origin, without applying a percentage of food price or charge per item. Duplicate notifications or repeated processing cannot duplicate a unit event. No automatic refund on later cancellation; correct provably erroneous, duplicate or unauthorised charges through governed adjustments. Consumption is independent of readiness/physical fulfilment.

~**US$0.05 for one accepted order / 20 orders per US$1** is a hypothesis only. Third-party WhatsApp response/interaction costs must be assessed separately from charging, with future transparent fair-use safeguards if needed. The precise unit amount, pricing, expiry, allowance and tax terms remain open.

**New Founder-approved continuity allowance: 10 recoverable consumption units per organisation.** Businesses/customers already in a valid ordering or engagement journey must not be abruptly cut off merely because the subscription expires or prepaid consumption is exhausted. PEWA permits **bounded completion of already-admitted valid requests and necessary order-status/exception communications**, with accepted orders consuming the available recoverable allowance as needed. The 10-unit allowance is **recoverable**, not free promotional usage; consumption debt is visible and recoverable upon replenishment/reactivation. Once ordinary allowance and the bounded recoverable allowance are exhausted, PEWA disallows additional chargeable acceptance except where an independent exceptional remedy is required by applicable obligations. No new structured-order journeys should be admitted under a commercially suspended or insufficient-balance organisation; existing commitments remain accessible for permitted resolution.

**Necessary reconciliation:** “10 recoverable units before a menu is restricted” means **ordering and subscription-dependent new engagement are subject to those continuity boundaries**, while a *valid, paid base menu subscription* does not vanish solely because ordering units run out. If the **base subscription** itself expires beyond grace, public menu visibility may be restricted, but customers already participating in an admitted journey retain bounded access to finish or view their order. Whether a limited short continuation of general public menu visibility is needed to satisfy the Founder's phrase “before a menu is restricted” remains an explicit Founder review question; 10 units do not silently convert to an extension of general menu-publication rights.

## 12. Free business offers and paid Featured Offers

Businesses can freely feature their own specials, seasonal offers or promotions within their **own subscribed business-menu experience** under ordinary publishing rules. Such offers are not automatically featured on PEWA Home.

Businesses may separately purchase **Featured Offers** targeting PEWA Home with business-selected relevant offerings, contextual category, geography, period, budget and placement conditions. The product preference is business self-service and governed automated allocation, with Operator Console oversight. Ordinary organic results cannot be purchased. Contextual, location and time-based targeting are included; behavioural targeting based on personal histories is not implicitly authorised.

**Exposure-based campaign commercial rule:** the purchased measure is **qualifying delivered exposure/impressions**, within campaign duration and spending limits, not conversion, actual orders, sales or fulfilment. A customer reaching a screen **where the promoted feature had an opportunity to be seen** may qualify as one exposure if the promotion was actually presented in the applicable visible display context; **mere landing on an unrelated page, a hidden/off-screen placement, or purchasing a campaign is not proof of exposure**. Detailed viewability, de-duplication and validity rules are deferred, and reports must distinguish qualified impressions from observed clicks/engagement without guaranteeing any conversions.

Campaign charging must account for delivered exposure, purchased/remaining entitlement, eligibility, suspension, expiry and credits. Suspension pauses new exposure, preserves undelivered eligible campaign value under disclosed terms, and never retroactively transforms undelivered impressions into delivered ones. No campaign promises sales or conversions. Prices and exposure-unit definitions remain to be approved.

## 13. Usage reporting, privacy and Operator Console

Business insights concern **PEWA-observed** menu visits, offering views, customer engagement, submitted/accepted PEWA orders, consumption, promotion impressions and actual platform activity; do not invent external sales, payment collection, physical fulfilment or food quality outcomes. Scope reporting to authorised roles and legitimate privacy rights.

Public menu information is distinct from private organisation records, staff permissions, guest/room information and customer order details. Optional identity, local recents, cross-device histories, sensitive recovery, deletion, consent, export and lawful retention need explicit applicable rules. PEWA may preserve truthful event meaning without indefinitely keeping personal data.

The **separate PEWA Operator Console**, to be assembled last, covers participation/eligibility, billing/renewals, adjustments, usage and promotional disputes, sponsored-exposure oversight, integrity/complaints, relevant reporting and narrow authorised support. Distinguish business unpublication, commercial suspension and platform enforcement. Trace exceptional operator actions. No general operator authority to alter merchant prices, decide order acceptance, declare Ready or assert fulfilment.
