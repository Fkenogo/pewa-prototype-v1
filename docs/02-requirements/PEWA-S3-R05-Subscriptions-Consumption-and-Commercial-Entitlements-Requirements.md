## 1. Commercial proposition and invariants

**Founder-approved 18 October model:** PEWA offers **one standard monthly organisation subscription covering menu listing and 200 included accepted-order consumption units**, irrespective of whether the business enables ordering. When included/available units are exhausted, the business may purchase additional unit bundles. **Enterprise** offers separately negotiated/custom terms for larger organisations. **Featured Offer exposure remains a separate purchase.** These structural refinements were approved on 18 October 2026; they do not imply engineering authorisation. An organisation can publish and maintain many legitimate brands, locations and menus without artificial per-menu or per-brand base fees.

**Illustrative pricing only:** US$10 per standard month including 200 units; add-on units at US$0.05 each, offered in US$10 bundles (200 units per bundle at these example prices). **None of these prices, exchange rates, taxes or enterprise terms is final.** PEWA business-facing prices display primarily in locally applicable currency, USD optionally. Meal-order payments remain directly between customer and business, outside PEWA.

Commercial changes may restrict new eligibility but cannot falsify historical business events, remove content/history as a punishment for non-payment or abandon valid already-admitted customer engagements. The **Founder-approved allowance is 10 recoverable consumption units per organisation**, not a promotional grant.

Terminology: **standard subscription** = monthly permission for subscribed public menu visibility/discovery/manual engagement **plus an included order-unit allocation**; **ordering enablement** = optional business decision to accept structured requests using available units, **not a separate plan purchase**; **consumption unit** = accepted-order usage event under approved terms; **recoverable allowance** = bounded permission to incur an outstanding usage obligation; **campaign entitlement** = purchased potential impression/exposure to be delivered, not an accepted order or subscription unit. Precise price and accounting policy is OPEN where stated.

## 2. Organisation subscription and published presence

### R05-001 — One accountable commercial organisation
PEWA **shall** associate base subscription standing and billing responsibility with the participating organisation, not force a separate base subscription for every menu, genuine location or related public brand.
- **Acceptance:** a hotel managing restaurant and room-service menus has one accountable organisation subscription; no automatic fee from an added legitimate menu.
- **Source:** S2 §§3,10; R01-007–010.

### R05-002 — Legitimate-presence safeguards
A broad organisation subscription **shall** remain subject to truthful identity and related-brand/publication eligibility, rather than permit indefinite unrelated businesses to share one commercial account.
- **Acceptance:** a fabricated duplicate brand can be restricted for integrity reasons; permitted legitimate brands are not numerically capped merely for price packaging.
- **Source:** S2 §§3,10; R01-008–011.

### R05-003 — Menu-only commercial completeness
An active eligible standard monthly subscription **shall** support permitted menu publishing, business-specific web presence, Directory discoverability, business-configured manual contact and offers on the business's own menu **without** ordering usage or PEWA Home promotion purchases.
- **Acceptance:** café publishes a seasonal discount on its own menu without paying for Featured Offers or ordering units.
- **Source:** S2 §§1,10,12; R02-018, R03-004.

### R05-004 — Optional ordering enablement under subscription
Optional structured ordering **shall** be available under an active standard or qualifying Enterprise subscription when the business explicitly enables it in relevant contexts and consumption capacity remains available. The standard monthly subscription includes units even for menu-only organisations; no separately purchased ordering plan is required. Payment alone shall not enable ordering.
- **Acceptance:** a hotel can keep ordering disabled on its public bar menu despite having ordering capability elsewhere.
- **Source:** S2 §§2,8,10; R04-004–005.

### R05-005 — Separated promotional purchase
Eligible businesses **shall** be able to purchase campaign exposure as a separately governed optional commercial service, not contingent on purchasing an ordering tier or privileged organic rank.
- **Acceptance:** a menu-only bakery can buy eligible Featured Offer exposure; it remains organically discoverable even when it buys none.
- **Source:** S2 §12; PD-08, PD-10.

### R05-006 — Explain available capabilities
Business administrators **shall** be able to understand active subscription eligibility, current ordering entitlement, permitted usage and optional campaign capability, without PEWA conflating them as one undifferentiated allowance.
- **Acceptance:** zero ordering units does not appear as an expired base menu subscription.
- **Source:** S2 §§10–11; R01-023–026.

## 3. Subscription states, payment continuity and reactivation

### R05-007 — Distinct commercial lifecycle
PEWA **shall** distinguish prepared/inactive, active, renewal-due, overdue within grace, commercially suspended, reactivated and closed relationships, separate from business-unpublished and platform-enforcement statuses.
- **Acceptance:** paying renewal can restore commercial standing, but cannot automatically remove an independent fraud restriction.
- **Source:** S2 §10; CP-10–12.

### R05-008 — Transparent renewal/overdue notice
The organisation **shall** receive understandable renewal, due/overdue, grace and suspension information, including what is currently available and what will change.
- **Acceptance:** admin can identify when public menu access may be suspended and where to resolve payment.
- **OPEN:** durations, notice frequency, applicable billing period.
- **Source:** S2 §10; PD-10F.

### R05-009 — Bounded grace
A subscription **shall** have an explicit bounded grace treatment when overdue before the defined commercial suspension, subject to later approved terms.
- **Acceptance:** no surprise interpretation of merely “renewal due” as immediate content deletion.
- **OPEN:** grace length and exception eligibility.
- **Source:** S2 §10; PD-10F.

### R05-010 — Post-grace public restriction
After applicable grace is exhausted, PEWA **shall** restrict newly accessible subscribed public menus, eligible Directory presence and new subscription-dependent engagement while conveying neutral unavailability at old menu destinations.
- **Acceptance:** suspended café does not accept new customers/orders through PEWA; old URL does not silently redirect to another merchant.
- **Source:** S2 §10; R02-031, R03-007.

### R05-011 — Preserve business content and history
A commercial lapse **shall not** delete or falsely overwrite the organisation's profiles, drafts, menus, previously published item content, permitted media references, historical orders, consumption records or campaign obligations.
- **Acceptance:** once reactivated the café can republish/restore its appropriate previous presence without rebuilding; lawful privacy deletion is still possible.
- **Source:** S2 §§10–11,13; CP-10.

### R05-012 — Workspace access during suspension
Authorised business account holders **shall** retain appropriately protected access to commercial standing, renewal/top-up, business settings, saved content, permitted historic activity and necessary existing-order resolution even where new publication is restricted.
- **Acceptance:** overdue café can update payment standing and view relevant order history.
- **Source:** S2 §§3,10–11; R01-025.

### R05-013 — Reactivation is not re-onboarding
Restoring acceptable payment standing **shall** restore the organisation's eligible subscribed capabilities without a blanket requirement to re-enter profiles or recreate menus, subject to current configured visibility, entitlement and distinct enforcement conditions.
- **Acceptance:** previously hidden menu is not forced public simply because subscription renewed.
- **Source:** S2 §10; PD-12.

### R05-014 — Closure distinct from nonpayment
Voluntary closure, account termination, business unpublication and integrity enforcement **shall** have distinct meanings, customer access effects, restoration conditions and appropriate record-retention treatment; nonpayment alone must not imply final content erasure.
- **Acceptance:** administrator can distinguish subscription suspension from an operator-imposed fraud listing restriction.
- **Source:** S2 §§10,13; CP-10–12.

## 4. Accepted-order consumption

### R05-015 — Acceptance, not submission
A valid, explicit authorised **business acceptance** of a PEWA structured order **shall** create the commercially chargeable accepted-order consumption event; submission, decline, withdrawal, expiry, Ready and physical fulfilment alone shall not.
- **Acceptance:** pending and declined orders do not consume; Accepted does even if no Ready follows.
- **Source:** S2 §§8,11; R04-015–020,031.

### R05-016 — One acceptance, one unit
One valid acceptance **shall** consume one ordering unit independently of basket size, items, optional extras, total order monetary amount or originating supported channel.
- **Acceptance:** five items in one accepted order trigger one unit; duplicate acceptance notifications do not trigger another.
- **Source:** S2 §11; R04-031,040.

### R05-017 — Channel-neutral core price meaning
PEWA **shall** treat valid accepted web and permitted WhatsApp-originated orders with the same fundamental unit semantics, while separately distinguishing third-party transport/messaging expense.
- **Acceptance:** no hidden second consumption event solely because the same order was communicated in another channel.
- **Source:** S2 §§9,11; PD-06.

### R05-018 — Available balance clarity
An entitled business **shall** be able to see ordinary available order units, usage events, bounded recoverable allowance used/remaining and outstanding recoverable obligations distinctly.
- **Acceptance:** the administrator can distinguish five prepaid units from two already-used recoverable units and cannot mistake debt for prepaid balance.
- **Source:** S2 §11; Founder ten-unit refinement.

### R05-019 — Early depletion notice
PEWA **shall** inform responsible administrators that ordering units are low or exhausted and explain impact on admitting new structured requests and resolving existing ones.
- **Acceptance:** business knows menu browsing may continue even when order submission is restricted.
- **Source:** S2 §§10–11; PD-10G.

### R05-020 — Insufficient units block new admission
Where no ordinary units or permitted authorisation to admit new structured orders remains, PEWA **shall** block new structured submissions while preserving subscribed base-menu access and valid previously admitted commitments.
- **Acceptance:** active café remains searchable, but new order submission clearly indicates ordering temporarily unavailable.
- **Source:** S2 §§10–11; R04-033.

### R05-021 — Preserve valid outstanding order resolution
Already-admitted pending orders and relevant post-acceptance order commitments **shall** remain accessible and resolvable under bounded continuity even after base subscription lapse or unit depletion.
- **Acceptance:** business can explicitly accept or decline eligible previously submitted requests without silently losing them when balance changes.
- **Source:** S2 §§10–11; R04-034–035.

### R05-022 — Exactly ten recoverable units
Each organisation **shall** have a bounded maximum of **10 recoverable accepted-order consumption units** under the Founder-approved continuity principle. They are recoverable obligations, not a free grant, and shall be applied under explicit policy to eligible already-admitted orders.
- **Acceptance:** an eligible prior pending request can be accepted while a recoverable unit remains, and the resulting obligation is visible; policy cannot silently grant unlimited negative usage.
- **OPEN:** effective reset period/trigger, concurrent requests, exhaustion dispute and collection/repayment mechanics, without changing approved ten-unit maximum.
- **Source:** S2 §11; Founder 17 Oct refinement.

### R05-023 — Existing engagement not abruptly severed
Commercial restrictions **shall not** abruptly sever protected status access, already-admitted request decisions, customer withdrawal/cancellation communications or other necessary actions to resolve existing PEWA commitments where permitted.
- **Acceptance:** customer can revisit order status after business subscription lapses; no new customer order starts as a consequence.
- **Source:** S2 §§10–11; R04-035.

### R05-024 — No silent debt forgiveness or limitless borrowing
Use of recoverable units **shall** generate attributable recovery obligations; PEWA **shall** not imply they are free, permit unbounded borrowing, or silently erase them on subscription downgrade, lapse or reactivation.
- **Acceptance:** after 10 allowed recoverable acceptances, the business is informed of the exhausted allowance and must restore standing under agreed terms before further eligible chargeable actions.
- **Source:** S2 §11; R04-034,037.

### R05-025 — No automatic reversal for later cancellation
Business-accepted orders remain chargeable after post-acceptance cancellation, absent a separately justified correction for invalid, duplicate or erroneous acceptance usage.
- **Acceptance:** two distinct events retained: accepted-order consumption and later cancellation; a valid refund/credit correction, if authorised, is explicitly recorded.
- **Source:** S2 §§8,11; R04-025,032.

### R05-026 — Accountable usage correction
PEWA **shall** support governed correction of incorrect, duplicate or unauthorised usage and preserve both original and correcting event meaning; routine business order decisions remain outside operator authority.
- **Acceptance:** an incorrect duplicated charge can be corrected without deleting the true underlying order acceptance.
- **Source:** S2 §§11,13; CP-10/12.

### R05-027 — Unit expiry/validity disclosed
If ordering units are subject to expiration, duration or carry-forward restrictions, those terms **shall** be disclosed before purchase and represented accurately in balances and usage.
- **OPEN:** whether expiry is permitted and on what terms.
- **Source:** S2 §11; commercial terms open.

## 5. Currency, charges and business-to-PEWA payment meaning

### R05-028 — Local currency first
PEWA subscription/ordering/campaign prices presented to participating businesses **shall** default to relevant supported local currency; USD may be optionally shown as a reference and shall not silently replace the amount actually payable.
- **Acceptance:** a Burundi business sees applicable BIF-denominated commercial offer where supported; optional USD is clearly reference-only unless separately selectable payable currency.
- **Source:** S2 §5; Founder currency direction.

### R05-029 — No unapproved rates
PEWA **shall** use only separately Founder-approved actual rates, periods, local-currency equivalents, taxes and eligibility terms; a US$10/month basic ceiling and US$0.05/acceptance **remain directional references**, not fixed authorised charges.
- **Acceptance:** business is never invoiced at an illustrative numerical rate solely because it appeared in a Product Definition.
- **Source:** S2 §§5,10–11; S2 closure.

### R05-030 — Clear business charges
The organisation **shall** see the commercially relevant meaning of subscription due, monthly included units and renewal allocation, accepted-order usage, prepaid units, recoverable obligations and promotional spend as distinguishable financial items.
- **Acceptance:** operator report cannot label a paid impression purchase as an accepted-order charge.
- **Source:** S2 §§10–12; PD-10J.

### R05-031 — Business-to-PEWA payment allowed
PEWA **may** facilitate permitted subscription, ordering-unit and Featured Offer payments made by the organisation to PEWA; this **shall not** imply PEWA processing customers' meal payments, deposits, refunds or settlement.
- **Acceptance:** subscription renewal has no effect on how a customer pays the restaurant for food.
- **Source:** S2 §§1,8,10; R04-030.

### R05-032 — Historical commercial meaning
The organisation **shall** be able to distinguish original commercial periods, usage and currency amounts from later adjustments, renewals or policy changes; PEWA **shall not** rewrite historical prices/usage meaning retrospectively.
- **Acceptance:** past accepted-order charge remains associated with then-applicable commercial terms after a future price change.
- **Source:** S2 §§5,10–11; CP-10.

### R05-033 — Clear renewal and purchase consent
Commercial purchase or top-up **shall** require attributable authorised business action and clear applicable amount, currency, period/units and material conditions before commitment.
- **Acceptance:** ordinary menu editing does not silently purchase ordering units or paid featuring.
- **Source:** S2 §§3,10–12; R01-018–021.

## 6. Promotion purchase boundary

### R05-034 — Promotion is a separate entitlement
Featured Offer exposure **shall** have purchase/budget and remaining entitlement distinct from base subscription and accepted-order consumption.
- **Acceptance:** buying featured exposure does not provide additional ordering units.
- **Source:** S2 §12; R06.

### R05-035 — Exposure, not sales
Purchased campaign commercial value **shall** be assessed against qualifying delivered exposure/impressions, not promised clicks, orders, revenue, conversions or fulfilment.
- **Acceptance:** an eligible on-screen opportunity to see can qualify under later agreed criteria; PEWA does not guarantee conversion.
- **Source:** S2 §12; Founder campaign disposition.

### R05-036 — Preserve undelivered eligible entitlement
If a campaign pauses because of subscription suspension or another governed eligibility restriction, already-delivered impressions remain accounted for and eligible undelivered purchase value **shall** be preserved subject to clear reactivation/expiry/correction rules.
- **Acceptance:** merely scheduling a featured placement during suspension is not billed as delivered exposure.
- **Source:** S2 §12; PD-12B. Detailed R06 policy governs.

### R05-037 — Advertisers cannot buy organic rank
Promotional entitlement **shall not** give the business superior organic Directory search ranking or override publication eligibility.
- **Acceptance:** non-advertising café still appears on relevant unpaid results.
- **Source:** S2 §§6,12; R03-026–027.

## 7. Accountability, permissions and exception controls

### R05-038 — Authorised commercial access
Only permitted organisation roles **shall** view sensitive billing balances, undertake commercial purchases, change entitlements or request financial corrections; an ordinary menu editor does not automatically possess these authorities.
- **Acceptance:** service attendant can resolve an authorised existing order without viewing organisation-wide payment records.
- **Source:** S2 §§3,10,13; R01-018–021.

### R05-039 — Bounded operator correction
PEWA operators **may** resolve authorised billing/consumption/account discrepancies with attributable reasons and appropriate review, without routine access to or control of merchant price setting, order acceptance or physical fulfilment.
- **Acceptance:** correcting a duplicate unit does not alter original business-declared Accepted event.
- **Source:** S2 §13; CP-08/12.

### R05-040 — Product reporting, not assumed revenue
Commercial reports **shall** distinguish actual PEWA billing/accepted-order usage/paid delivered promotion from externally unobserved customer meal sales, collection and delivery.
- **Acceptance:** an accepted-order unit is not presented as PEWA-verified restaurant revenue.
- **Source:** S2 §13; R01-027.

## 8. Proposed standard and Enterprise subscription refinements (pending approval)

### R05-041 — Standard monthly included units
Each standard organisation subscription renewal **shall** provide an included allocation of **200 accepted-order units** under the proposed product structure, whether or not the business elects to use structured ordering. Units are consumed only upon valid business acceptance, not menu listing or browsing.
- **Acceptance:** a menu-only café with an active standard subscription has eligible published menus and 200 included units available should it later enable ordering during the relevant subscription period.
- **Approved COM-D01:** 200 included units are granted on successful monthly renewal; unused included units expire at the period boundary. Detailed allocation timing, recovery offset and accounting rules still require explicit policy.
- **Source:** Founder proposed refinement, 18 October 2026.

### R05-042 — Standard top-up bundles
An organisation with eligible structured ordering **shall** be able to acquire additional accepted-order capacity via separately purchased unit bundles without changing its standard subscription. **Illustrative** package: US$10 per 200 additional units at US$0.05 per unit; final prices/pack sizes are not approved.
- **Acceptance:** after using its included 200 units, a business can purchase an eligible top-up while its monthly listing subscription remains current.
- **Approved COM-D02/D03:** unused purchased top-up units persist across a monthly renewal, subject to separately disclosed validity terms; consume included units first, then purchased units, then eligible bounded recoverable units. Actual prices, bundle denomination and exact validity/expiry remain unapproved.
- **Source:** Founder proposed refinement, 18 October 2026.

### R05-043 — Negotiated Enterprise arrangement
PEWA **shall** permit a separately agreed Enterprise commercial arrangement for larger businesses, with configurable subscription/volume terms, while retaining platform truth, protected access and fair usage accounting.
- **Acceptance:** a large hotel group can negotiate commercial capacity without being forced into repeated standard packages; different contract pricing does not change order acceptance semantics.
- **OPEN:** eligibility thresholds, account negotiation, minimum commitments, Enterprise inclusions, invoicing and contract policy.
- **Source:** Founder proposed refinement, 18 October 2026.

## 9. Journeys and continuity scenarios

**Menu-only subscription:** organisation registers and pays applicable standard monthly subscription → publishes many legitimate menus/locations → customers access direct links and organic Directory → own-menu promotions allowed → no accepted-order consumption or paid Featured exposure required.

**Ordering opt-in:** business has active standard subscription with 200 included units, independently enables relevant contexts, and may top up as needed → customer submits request → business expressly accepts → one chargeable usage event; no charge for decline/withdrawal/expired unanswered request.

**Exhausted included and topped-up ordering:** business has no ordinary units but valid earlier submitted requests → up to ten remaining permitted recoverable acceptance units protect bounded commitments → new request admission limited under commercial policy → recoverable charges remain visible and owed; base menu stays visible when subscription current.

**Subscription lapses:** overdue → bounded grace → commercial suspension restricts new visibility/engagement but preserves content/history, authorised account access and protected resolution of valid prior commitments → restores eligible services after renewal, not by rebuilding profile.

**Campaign purchase:** optional purchase by eligible business → distinct delivered exposure account → pause if eligibility suspended; preserve undelivered eligible value under R06 rules; never infer clicks or conversions from purchasing impressions.

## 11. Approved COM-D01–D07 policy alignment (18 October 2026)

The [COM-001 decision record](PEWA-S3-COM-001-Subscription-and-Consumption-Policy-Decision-Brief.md) is approved and controls these details:

- **D01:** Each successful standard monthly renewal grants 200 included accepted-order units. Unused monthly included units expire at that period boundary. Business menu-only use does not consume units.
- **D02:** Purchased top-up units persist through ordinary monthly renewal; exact validity period, closure treatment and permitted credits/refunds require separate approved terms. Units can be used for new structured orders only when subscription and business enablement permit.
- **D03:** Consume monthly included units first, then paid top-up units, then eligible bounded recoverable units; report each source distinctly.
- **D04:** Ten recoverable units are a maximum outstanding organisation liability, not ten newly free units on every renewal. Their use serves eligible already-admitted commitments; recovery/offset, exact concurrency and disputes remain open.
- **D05:** Renewal due, grace, suspension and reactivation are separate; suspension blocks new public/eligible engagement after applicable grace but preserves content, existing-order resolution and authorised workspace. Top-up does not replace subscription renewal. Grace duration remains open.
- **D06:** Enterprise may negotiate prices/volumes/service terms but may not waive protected customer access, truthful order states, historic record meaning or PEWA’s non-merchant boundary.
- **D07:** Business commercial presentation shall show one standard offer with included 200 units, distinct top-ups/recoverable balance and enabled contexts, never inventing consumed units for menu-only businesses.

All dollar examples remain illustrative; no final BIF/USD rates, tax or payment provider is approved. Any earlier R05 wording suggesting a separate paid ordering-tier selection is superseded by the approved standard-includes-units model.
