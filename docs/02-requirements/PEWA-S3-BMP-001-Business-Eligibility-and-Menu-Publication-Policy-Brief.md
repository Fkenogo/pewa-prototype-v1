# PEWA-S3-BMP-001 — Business Eligibility, Menu Publication and Availability Policy Decision Brief

**Status:** FOUNDER APPROVED — BMP-D01–D08, 18 October 2026 (PR #33); remaining numeric/legal details still open  
**Date:** 18 October 2026  
**Stage:** Stage 3 IN PROGRESS  
**Sources:** Approved Constitution, S2 Product Definition, R01 Business Foundation, R02 Menu Management, R03 Customer Discovery, R07 Customer Protection, R08 Operator Console. Earlier food-taxonomy research remains informative, not binding.

## Settled principles

PEWA supports legitimate food-service organisations, their related brands and locations, and numerous menus without arbitrary menu/brand subscription fees. Businesses author descriptions, regional-language text, prices, publication and availability. Business categorisation aids searchability and may use custom terms. PEWA owns integrity and fair disclosure, not culinary or fulfilment verification. Public and protected hotel menus remain separate. These are **not** reopened.

## Founder-approved policy directions

### BMP-D01 — Minimum viable business identity and first publication
**Approved direction:** Permit a proportionate fast path to first useful published menu once an organisation identifies an accountable owner, a truthful display identity and usable service/contact context and acknowledges responsibility for content. Require enhanced evidence only where risk or the declared business structure warrants it. Do not demand a full corporate dossier from every micro-bakery.

**Confirm:** minimum business fields by jurisdiction, whether personal traders are admissible, verification/appeal triggers, permitted initial provisional publication.
**Acceptance:** a real small café with a straightforward menu can publish without fabricating a tax registration; an impersonated brand cannot evade platform review.

### BMP-D02 — Related brands, venues and delivery-only businesses
**Approved direction:** Permit genuinely affiliated brands and service contexts under one accountable organisation; distinguish customer-visitable location from declared delivery zone. Additions of unusually many unrelated brand identities prompt eligibility review rather than arbitrary per-brand fee or automatic rejection.

**Confirm:** relationship evidence, brand transfer, business acquisition and disputed brand claims.
**Acceptance:** a hotel may manage a public café and protected room-service menu, and a delivery kitchen may state its service area without revealing a private preparation address.

### BMP-D03 — Menu publication permission
**Approved direction:** Separate *content editing* from *public publication/withdrawal* as distinguishable permissions in the product; organisation administrators may delegate both to the same trusted member, but the product never treats all content editors as authorised to publish by default.

**Alternative:** publication inherent in editor role unless administrator revokes.
**Acceptance:** a staff member may prepare a menu draft without accidentally exposing it.

### BMP-D04 — Customer-facing minimum item information
**Approved direction:** Require enough honest information to identify a public offering, its supplying business, applicable service context and price/currency where a price is offered; show availability as the business declares it. Do **not** require a shared food-class code, English translation, elaborate description or photo as a condition of publication. Applicable mandatory consumer disclosures require competent legal determination.

**Acceptance:** an “Ibiharage” item with a Kirundi description and BIF price is publishable without an English equivalent or mandatory classification.

### BMP-D05 — Optional classifications and local-language search
**Approved direction:** Business-authored category/collection, title and description remain primary. Optional shared classifications and provisional synonyms improve discovery but cannot overrule displayed names or silently infer ingredients, allergens or certifications. Maintain a correction path for misleading search mappings.

**Acceptance:** “Maharage,” “Ibiharage” and “Beans” can be represented as business-authored terms; no unknown cross-language equivalence is asserted as verified.

### BMP-D06 — Reusable offerings and multi-menu changes
**Approved direction:** Businesses may reuse a common offering across several menus, but materially affecting published price, currency, availability or customer-visible terms in another menu requires clear scope preview and explicit authorised confirmation. Local menu-specific overrides retain their meaning; historical accepted orders retain snapshots.

**Confirm:** the precise “material” change list and reasonable batch publication policy.
**Acceptance:** editing a shared pastry cannot silently change a hotel room-service menu price without a visible confirmation of affected menus.

### BMP-D07 — Availability precedence and time interpretation
**Approved direction:** PEWA shows the business's effective availability for the relevant menu/context and requested service time, respecting deliberate specific override ahead of a general recurring schedule. If declarations conflict or are insufficient, PEWA should avoid representing an item as definitely orderable; manual browsing/contact can remain where publication is authorised.

**Confirm:** precedence amongst context-level restriction, item exception, dated override, recurring schedule and emergency pause; time-zone handling and conflict messaging. Do not invent a final ordering algorithm now.
**Acceptance:** a business explicitly pausing an item for today cannot accidentally accept a future-order request for today's paused period simply because the regular weekly schedule says open.

### BMP-D08 — Moderation and legitimate redress
**Approved direction:** PEWA operators may restrict demonstrably misleading or unsafe platform presentation or impersonation with proportional notice and review, without routinely editing merchants' food facts or making their order decisions. Provide correction and appeal, including for erroneous geography/classification or false-positive identity flags.

**Confirm:** evidence thresholds, emergency restrictions, appeal windows, legal/safety reporting obligations.
**Acceptance:** fraudulent restaurant identity is suspended with attributable reason; a genuine business can contest an error.

## Terms and platform policy allocation

- **Business Terms:** identity truthfulness, related-brand eligibility, authority to publish, business responsibility for offering facts, prices, availability, claims and service delivery, enforcement and appeals.
- **Customer-facing terms/notices:** source of menu information, business-declared availability, order-request semantics, safety and certification uncertainty.
- **Privacy notice:** public business identity versus private contact/guest/dispatch addresses; legitimate publication access.
- **Operational policy:** staff publishing permissions, optional classification governance, report handling and appeals.
- Final binding legal disclosures and jurisdiction-specific obligations require competent review; this package is not a published T&C draft.
