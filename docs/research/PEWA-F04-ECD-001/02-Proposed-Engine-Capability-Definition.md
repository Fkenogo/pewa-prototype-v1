# Proposed PEWA Engine Capability Definition

**Disposition requested:** Founder review; proposal only
**Applicable foundation authority:** F01, F02 FD-01–FD-05, and F03 PAP-01–PAP-12 subject to six conditions

## Definition

The PEWA engine is the product-level capability boundary through which the PEWA product supports businesses in defining and publishing food, beverage and meal-related offerings, gives people persistent web access and discovery, facilitates the approved manual customer-engagement mode, and, where a business is entitled and chooses to enable it, supports structured customer selection or order submission and communication of recorded business decisions. The two approved F01 operating modes (menu access with manual engagement; digital ordering) are each governed by subscription entitlement.

The engine is responsible for product behavior and integrity within that boundary. It does not become the food provider, set business facts, accept orders on behalf of a business, prepare or deliver food, or turn optional channels into authoritative product state.

This definition is a capability-level proposal. It does not specify internal modules, data models, services, APIs, deployments or technical ownership.

## Capability cohesion and separation test

The candidate areas are related by the F01 proposition but do not all necessarily constitute one Miledge Enduring Capability or one Miledge Shared Platform. A through B describe offering definition, publication and access; C through D apply only when a business enables ordering; E through H establish variation, industry boundaries, channel independence and product obligations.

Keep them together as a PEWA product-level assessment unless later evidence shows materially different recurring needs, separate reuse across multiple products, distinct authority or lifecycle, or separate business meaning. That later evidence may justify separating capability assessments. It must not be translated directly into engine implementation decomposition.

## Explicit exclusions

The proposed engine does not include general commerce, multi-business checkout, restaurant POS, inventory, procurement, restaurant management, staff scheduling, kitchen control, food quality assurance, physical service, fulfillment operations, delivery logistics, independent readiness verification or a general review network. Ratings and AI-authored authoritative facts remain outside approved scope.
